# =====================================================================
# OUTLOOK ON THE JEWS, on the character window and in opinion (V35,
# 2026-10-13). Daniel: "opinion of Jews run from -50 to +50 ... directly
# on opinion". A lord's opinion of every Jewish character (vanilla
# judaism_religion_opinion) equals his outlook on the Jews, capped at
# +/-50, in steps of 5 (a modifier holds a fixed number, so one per step;
# truncated toward zero so the step's band name matches the band).
# Typical lords (outlook +5..+45) offset much of vanilla's faith-
# hostility penalty (astray -10, hostile -20, evil -30); cold and hostile
# ones add to it. Each step is named by its band ("Outlook on the Jews:
# Well-disposed") and shown in the opinion breakdown with its value.
# kehillah_outlook_marker_refresh_effect keeps the right one on each
# landed Christian or Muslim ruler. A vanilla .gui can't be extended
# piece by piece (docs/spec/gui-spike-community-list.md), so a character
# modifier is how this shows without forking the character window.
# Knock-on: a host's opinion of a community leader carries his outlook
# (the host-attitude opinion tiers, the review's patron trigger).
# =====================================================================
