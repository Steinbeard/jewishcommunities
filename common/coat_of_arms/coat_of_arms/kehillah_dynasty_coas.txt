# V51 (2026-10-21): the houses on the 867, 1066 and 1178 start screens.
# Charges are editorial, chosen from a name or a role, not from any arms
# these families bore: the harp for the Kalonymide poets, the lamp of
# learning for Alfasi, the scribe's penbox for the Gaon,
# the rosette for Oria, the serpent of
# Dan (Genesis 49:17) for Eldad, the fish of the Sea of Galilee for the
# Masoretes, the key of authority for the Gaon of
# Baghdad, the star for the Tosafist, the moon of hidden things for the
# Raavad, and the lily (shoshan) for Ibn Shoshan.
