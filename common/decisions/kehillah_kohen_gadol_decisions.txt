# Restore the Kohen Gadol (docs/spec/2026-11-15-headship-offices-and-tenet-gates.md,
# section 3). The step after Restore Jerusalem's Temple Service: the ruler of
# Jerusalem seats a High Priest. A courtier Kohen is seated through a temple
# holding in the county (vanilla makes a temple's holder a theocratic vassal),
# then receives a landless High Priesthood. A ruler who is himself a Kohen may
# take the office as priest-king (the Hasmonean pattern). The High Priesthood
# heads Rabbinic Judaism only when the faith has no head; otherwise it sits
# under the existing head.
