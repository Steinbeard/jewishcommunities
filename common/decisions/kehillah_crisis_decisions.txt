# The crisis window's actions (situation_decisions of kehillah_community_crisis). Since 2026-11-01
# there are three, the same in every phase and every kind of crisis
# (docs/spec/2026-11-01-three-standing-actions.md). Each opens a scene whose options are the
# sub-actions (events/kehillah_crisis_act_events.txt). The sub-actions share their checks and
# effects with Military's Community Safety buttons (common/scripted_guis/kehillah_crisis_gui.txt).
# Each is shown only to the leader of a community whose race is running, so another community's
# leader or an observer sees none of them.
