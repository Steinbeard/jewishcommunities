# Jewish Communities

A Crusader Kings III mod about Jewish diaspora communities, built around the Kehillah of Worms bookmark and a playable non-landed community government.

## Current Version

- Mod version: `0.1`
- CK3 version: `1.19.0.6` (`Scribe`)
- Current scenario: Kehillah of Worms, 1066

## Current Features

- A playable Kehillah government for a landless Jewish community
- Community quarter buildings, including a synagogue, yeshiva, countinghouse, hekdesh, mikvah, sofer's workshop, slaughterhouse, markets, and craft workshops
- Chief Rabbi, Treasurer, Shtadlan, and minor communal positions
- Appointment-based community leadership and succession systems
- Community decisions for mediation, charity, and scholarly work
- Jewish holiday flavor events with seasonal scheduling
- Patrilineal Kohen and Levite traits with small monthly prestige bonuses
- Custom historical and generated Jewish dynasty names

## Installation

1. Copy or clone this repository into the CK3 mod directory:

   `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod/jewishcommunities`

2. Create or enable a launcher entry for the mod using `descriptor.mod`.
3. Enable **Jewish Communities** in the CK3 launcher.
4. Start the Kehillah of Worms bookmark to play the current scenario.

The mod targets CK3 `1.19.0.6`. Other game versions may require script or GUI updates.

## Design

The community is organized around three long-term outcomes:

- **Prosperity**: population, economic capacity, buildings, stewardship, and community income.
- **Stability**: internal cohesion, dispute resolution, religious unity, and protection from external threats.
- **Greatness**: learning, piety, prestige, scholarship, books, and reputation among other communities.

The proposed pillar model is documented in [docs/spec/v1-kehillah-community.md](docs/spec/v1-kehillah-community.md). The roadmap and implementation status are in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and [docs/implementation/v1-kehillah-implementation.md](docs/implementation/v1-kehillah-implementation.md).

## Status

The Kehillah baseline is under active development. The government and initial Worms scenario have been tested in-game. The newer building, holiday, trait, derived-pillar, and health-UI work should be treated as work in progress until separately playtested.
