# V51 bookmark seats (docs/spec/v51-bookmark-rosters.md), 2026-10-21.
#
# Same shape as kehillah_world_titles.txt: a founded-year anchor, a holder
# on the bookmark date, and holder = 0 a generation later so the title
# does not carry the named leader into a later bookmark. Holders are in
# history/characters/kehillah_v51_bookmark_characters.txt.
#
# Founded years are the conservative anchors that file's header describes:
#   Fez        809  the Idrisid foundation of the city; Jews are attested
#                   in Fez from its first generations.
#   Oria       800  the Chronicle of Ahimaaz places Shefatiah's family in
#                   Oria for generations before 867; no earlier secure date.
#   Lucca      800  Kalonymide Lucca before the move to Mainz; no secure
#                   founding date, so a round 9th-century anchor.
#   Tiberias    20  the city's foundation; Jewish from the start.
#   Posquieres 1150 the Raavad's academy there; the community is older,
#                   but not securely dated.
# =====================================================================
