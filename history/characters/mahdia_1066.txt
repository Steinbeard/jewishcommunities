# THE COMMUNITY. Kairouan's great academy -- Rabbenu Hananel (d. about
# 1055/56) and Nissim Gaon (d. 1062) -- was scattered when the Banu Hilal
# sacked Kairouan in 1057. The Zirids moved their capital to al-Mahdiyya
# on the coast, and Kairouan's Jews regrouped there and in Gabes, Sicily
# and Fustat; Jewish life in Kairouan itself did not recover. Mahdia is
# that small refugee community, starting weak. The ruined yeshiva hall
# of Kairouan stands on the map (kehillah_setup_mahdia_start_effect).
#
# THE LEADER. Labrat ben Moses ibn Sighmar appears in Cairo Geniza letters
# as dayyan (judge) of al-Mahdiyya around the middle of the 11th century.
# His exact dates are not established here; the birth year is estimated
# and he is placed in office in 1066 on the strength of those letters.
# Treat as a lightly attested figure, and check against a scholarly
# source (e.g. Goitein's Geniza studies) before quoting in event text.
#

# is the closest available approximation.
