# Community Safety as a planning page

The Military tab displays (1) the annual chance of an eligible incident, (2) court opposition and popular hostility, with a separate wealth/renown exposure line, and (3) stability and protection with navigation to actual levers. All exact component values stay in tooltips. A current crisis has only a card linking to its situation; its races, phase actions, readiness, diplomacy, and aftermath are no longer duplicated in Military.

The pressure rows are derived views of the existing danger terms, not extra multipliers. Court opposition adds the realm-seat term, a +10 term while the leader actually bears the Nagid's vizier service modifier, and a temporary local rivals term. The communal Nagid office alone adds no office penalty. Popular hostility adds recent faith incidents, hard times, host teaching, a badge, and a temporary local agitation term. Wealth and greatness remain separate exposure rather than being called popular opinion. Stability, the ruler's outlook, royal protection, a religious assurance, and the post-decrees lull lower the score. The director still rolls the same score divided by ten, up to 12% per eligible quarter.

The eligible quarterly roll has a 0.251% floor, which produces just over 1% over four rolls. Danger itself still floors at zero. Active crises and cooldowns prevent the director from offering a new incident; the displayed annual chance is the underlying chance if eligible, with the pause tagged separately. A successful roll can lead to several kinds of incident, not necessarily an attack.

Granada's 1066 setup gives the community +25 temporary court rivalry and +8 local agitation for two years. Both represent pressure already present before Abu Ishaq's poem and expire if unrenewed. The vizier service later supplies its generic +10. With the screenshot's other terms (+17 Prosperity, +7 Greatness, -27 Stability, -8 ruler outlook, +5 seat), this gives danger 27 before vizier service and 37 after it, roughly 10% and 14% annual incident chance, both Tense. Badis remains well disposed. Granada's authored crisis starts independently of the director; this background calculation does not alter its scheduled onset.

The existing Bet Din activity and Petition the Host task are linked from the page. A new Shtadlan task, Seek Religious Support, appears when the host has a court chaplain. At 1–4% per month by the Shtadlan's Diplomacy, it secures a nonstacking two-year public assurance: -8 background danger and +3 monthly Spared progress if an attack warning occurs while it lasts. It uses the same council slot as Petition the Host. This is a local adviser's public intervention, not a change to the host faith's teaching or a promise of soldiers. Its monthly effect and presentation need live verification.

Vanilla-style Important Actions also point to preparation once annual danger reaches Uneasy (3%): Petition the Host when the ruler's outlook or settlement policy could still improve, and Seek Religious Support when the host has a court chaplain and no assurance is in force. The Shtadlan must be appointed, and an assigned task removes its own recommendation. Both open Council, where the player chooses which task gets the shared seat. The existing Bet Din recommendation opens its activity planner. These preparation prompts give way to the existing crisis alert during an active crisis; a cooldown alone does not prevent planning.

The pressure terms, chance, and floor can be inspected with `event kehillah_inspector_debug.2`. New variables on the community title expire normally and require no save migration; an existing 1066 save whose initial setup already ran will not receive Granada's new seeds.
