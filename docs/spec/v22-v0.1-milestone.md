## 1. The goal

Within the next few weeks, reach a **v0.1** whose core promise is: *start in Ashkenaz in 1066 as
the leader of a Kehillah, and have a genuinely fun, legible game for 50+ years* (through the First
Crusade of 1096 and its aftermath). **The Jewish gameplay loop is the priority.** Host-side and
non-Jewish-player features matter, but second.

## 2. Three playstyle prototypes (the acceptance test for v0.1)

Each v0.1 feature should make at least one of these better. A 50-year playthrough of each should
have a beginning, a crisis, and a legacy.

1. **The Scholar — Rashi of Troyes (rabbinic).** Study Torah, write great works, train students,
   sit on Batei Din, while still leading a community. Mostly built already (study/learn Torah,
   write a book, Bet Din conference, library, V21 ordination paths spec). Gaps: a sense of
   *legacy* (students who become rabbis elsewhere, works that spread, responsa), and making the
   scholarship pay back into the community's pillars visibly.
2. **The Shtadlan in the storm — a Sh'um or Cologne leader (diplomatic).** Protect the community
   as the Crusades erupt: negotiate with popular crusaders, local lords, bishops, the Emperor, and
   the Jewish leadership network; bribes; refuge; flight; forced conversion and whether converts
   may return; mob violence and massacre; church stances; afterwards dirges/kinot, memorial, the
   eventual Hasidei Ashkenaz, and reconstruction. Must be **dynamic** — keyed to the Crusade
   actually being called and armies actually passing through, not a fixed 1096 date script.
3. **The Financier — a Jewish adventurer founding in England (economic).** Accept William the
   Conqueror's invitation, found a settlement, and profit from funding the economy and supporting
   royal administration. V18 already founds the English communities dynamically after the
   Conquest; the gap is a *player* path (an adventurer taking up the invitation) and an economic
   loop that makes royal finance the engine of the playthrough.

## 4. Design guidance already given (binding for builders)

- **Friendliness is multi-dimensional and visible.** Economic (does this ruler profit from Jews:
  loans, tolls, Jewish-run trade), personal (opinion of specific Jewish characters, traits like
  greedy/zealous/just/compassionate), religious (faith doctrines, the local church hierarchy's
  stance, crusade fervor), political (does protecting Jews assert the ruler's authority — e.g. the
  Emperor's own protection privileges). Consistent with V17: never one hidden hostility number.
  Whatever score exists must have a breakdown the player can read.
- **Expulsion is an event-led process, not a deletion.** V16/V17 already commit to: policy
  worsens one rung at a time, visibly; Banned starts a warning path; migration counterplay exists
  before expulsion becomes final. Expulsion should hand into the same teardown/migration path as
  dissolution and voluntary departure (feature 1) — one mechanism, three causes.
- **Crusade content is dynamic.** Hook the real crusade (vanilla Great Holy War) and real army
  movement where possible; historical 1096 names and places are flavour for the Rhineland case,
  not a date trigger.

## 5. Historical anchors for the Crusade chain (for the spec writer; verify before citing in-game)

- **Before:** 1084, Bishop Rüdiger Huozmann of Speyer invites Jews (after a fire in Mainz) with a
  walled quarter and charter; 1090, Henry IV's privileges for the Jews of Speyer and Worms. These
  are exactly the "charter" and "protection by a lord" dynamics the mod already models.
- **1096, the popular/Rhineland crusade bands** (Emicho of Flonheim and others), distinct from the
  princes' armies. Outcomes varied *by the local protector's choice*:
  - **Speyer (3 May):** Bishop Johann protected the community; about a dozen killed; he punished
    perpetrators. A "friendly protector" outcome.
  - **Worms (18 and 25 May):** those at home killed first; those sheltering in the bishop's palace
    killed a week later. Protection that failed.
  - **Mainz (27 May):** Archbishop Ruthard accepted payment for protection, then withdrew;
    Kalonymus ben Meshullam led the community; mass killing and martyrdom (*kiddush ha-Shem*).
    Bribes that bought nothing.
  - **Cologne (June):** Archbishop Hermann III dispersed Jews to nearby villages (Neuss, Xanten
    and others) for hiding; many were found and killed there. Flight/dispersal as a choice with
    risk.
  - **Trier, Regensburg:** forced mass baptisms.
- **After:** 1097, Henry IV permits the forcibly baptized to return to Judaism (the antipope
  Clement III objected) — a concrete "can converts revert" lever tied to the Emperor's disposition.
  Rashi's responsa are generally read as lenient toward returning forced converts. Chronicles
  (Solomon bar Samson, Eliezer bar Nathan, the Mainz Anonymous), kinot and the *Av HaRachamim*
  prayer, memorial books; reconstruction of the communities within years. The **Hasidei
  Ashkenaz** (Samuel he-Hasid, Judah he-Hasid, Eleazar of Worms) emerge in the mid-12th to 13th
  century, which is late-game for a 50-year v0.1 but a natural legacy hook. Papal *Sicut Judaeis*
  (Calixtus II, c. 1120) and Bernard of Clairvaux's intervention in the Second Crusade (1146) are
  later church-stance beats.
- **Tone.** This is atrocity content about real people. Write it with gravity; consider a game
  rule controlling depiction intensity. No gamified "massacre score."

### Vanilla 1.19 hooks available for the Crusade chain — research only, 2026-09-25 (Sukkot heartbeat 2)

S11 is DANIEL-REVIEW: nothing below is built or designed, it is the "which vanilla hooks exist"
survey that item explicitly allows. All of it was read out of the installed 1.19 game files, with
file and line, not recalled.

- **There is no "a crusade was declared" on_action.** The only code on_actions for Great Holy Wars
  are `on_potential_great_holy_war_invalidation`, `on_great_holy_war_invalidation`,
  `on_great_holy_war_countdown_end` and `on_great_holy_war_participant_replaced`
  (`common/on_action/religion_on_actions.txt:987-1021`; root is the GHW itself for the first three).
  The declaration and preparation beats are ordinary *events* fired from `on_faith_monthly` (root =
  the faith): `great_holy_war.0001` "the Sponsor declares a Great Holy War", `.0010` "GHWs are
  unlocked for a Faith", `.0003` "the preparation for the next GHW begins"
  (`religion_on_actions.txt:577-590`; `events/religion_events/great_holy_war_events.txt:37-40, 626`).
  **Implication:** to react to a crusade being *called*, the cheapest route is a check on the mod's
  own existing quarterly/yearly pulse (`exists = faith:catholic.great_holy_war`), not a new
  on_action hook. `on_great_holy_war_countdown_end` is the one clean hook for "the war actually
  launches now".
- **The current crusade is readable from script.** `<faith>.great_holy_war` is a real scope link,
  and from it `ghw_target_title` (the de jure kingdom targeted) and `ghw_attackers_strength` are
  real fields — all three used in vanilla's own `great_holy_war.0060` trigger
  (`great_holy_war_events.txt:4126-4170`) and in `on_great_holy_war_countdown_end`'s own trigger.
- **"Armies actually passing through" already has a hook, and vanilla uses it for exactly this
  purpose.** `on_army_enter_province` (`common/on_action/army_on_actions.txt:656`) gives root = the
  army's owner and `scope:army`, with `army_commander` and `location` readable off it.
  `great_holy_war.0060` — the hidden event that grants the Crusader trait when a host reaches the
  holy land — hangs off this hook and identifies a crusading army by `any_character_war = { using_cb
  = undirected_great_holy_war / directed_great_holy_war, is_attacker = root }`, also checking
  `any_liege_or_above` so a vassal marching under a crusading liege counts. That is a direct,
  precedented answer to V22 §4's "keyed to army movement, not a date": a Rhineland community can
  learn that a crusading host has entered its own county without any new machinery.
- **The 1096 popular bands have no vanilla analogue.** Emicho's mobs were not any ruler's levy and
  so cannot be GHW participants; if they are wanted as a threat distinct from the princes' armies,
  they have to be a mod-side construct (an event chain, or event troops) rather than something read
  off the GHW. Worth Daniel's decision, since it changes how much of the chain can ride on vanilla
  and how much has to be written from scratch.

## 6. What else v0.1 / v1 likely needs (Claude's recommendations, 2026-09-25)

**Approved by Daniel, 2026-09-25 (same day)**, with specifics: responsa, improving succession,
scholars being hired as Chief Rabbi and moving between communities, the three-character bookmark,
and community goals — all now in ROADMAP's Sukkot queue (S3-S7), alongside the soak test (S10).
He also directed that the Sukkot autonomous window focus on building and live-testing rather than
writing detailed specs; friendliness and the Crusades chain are held for design with him afterward.

1. **A 50-year soak test.** A scripted observe run (AI-only or player-parked, speed 5) that
   `debug_log`s every community's pillars and key events yearly, to find drift, dead loops, and
   runaway numbers. This is the fastest way to answer "is it fun/stable for 50 years" before
   humans play it, and it's automatable.
2. **A pacing arc of goals.** Right now there are many systems but few *objectives*. Community
   ambitions or era goals (build the yeshiva, earn a charter, survive the storm, rebuild) give 50
   years a shape.
3. **Family continuity.** Marriage matching within and between communities (shidduchim, dowries),
   and how dynasty prestige works under appointment succession — multi-generation play lives here.
4. **Inter-community life.** Letters/responsa between rabbis, scholar migration, trade credit
   between communities — makes the Sh'um network matter outside the Bet Din.
5. **Bookmark and onboarding.** A bookmark offering the three prototype characters (Rashi at
   Troyes, a Sh'um/Cologne leader, an adventurer near Rouen for England), a short in-game concept
   guide (game concepts / encyclopedia entries), and art for decisions/buildings (missing-icon
   warnings today).
6. **AI parity.** AI communities should visibly use the same systems so the world feels alive.
7. **Stability of the risky core.** The still-open `change_government` "illegal government"
   error on appointment succession, save/reload of charters, and founded-community succession
   should all be closed before a public v0.1.
8. **Release hygiene.** Game rules for sensitive content, compatibility notes, performance check
   of quarterly pulses at scale, Workshop page.
