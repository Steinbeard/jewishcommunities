# She'elot: being asked

## 1. Daniel's decisions (2026-11-22)

- **The reward of high Greatness is being asked.** Other communities bring their hard cases to the most authoritative
  posek they can reach.
- **Reuse the Bet Din docket** for the content of a she'elah. There is no new case system.
- **Responsa pass only between leaders of the same faith.** The same rule now governs Bet Din attendance: a court
  admits the host's faith, any rite (built 2026-11-22). A Karaite never sits on a Rabbinic court, nor writes to one.
- **The ruling community's influence can move a community toward any rite of their shared faith.**
- **Asking** gives a little Stability and the answerer's opinion, at the cost of the answerer's influence. A hook is
  too much (section 4).
- **Related changes, built the same day:**
  - the Synod's gate is Greatness Flourishing or higher;
  - AI mutual aid weighs the asking community's Greatness.

## 2. What exists already

The Bet Din has a bounded responsa loop (`events/kehillah_bet_din_sources_events.txt`, the `kehillah_din_sources.*`
chain):

- **Asking.** During a sitting, the court may *consult* a scholar elsewhere instead of hearing the case
  (`kehillah_din_consult`).
- **The letter.** The scholar gets the letter (`kehillah_din_letter_N`, "[community] asks your opinion: ..."), and may:
  - answer briefly (`kehillah_din_answer_N`);
  - answer carefully (`kehillah_din_answer_careful_N`), which needs a mastered work on the case's order of the Mishnah
    or Talmudic depth;
  - decline.
- **The reply** returns within 25 days and sets the quality of the court's ruling.

All 14 docket cases have letter and reply text. The 2026-11-22 audit fixed three problems:

- nine letters narrated the options;
- one phrase was awkward;
- two letters named the tractate in the question.

The letters otherwise read as letters, and they speak of "the court" as the asking community's own.

So the she'elah is this loop with three changes:

- it no longer needs a sitting;
- it chooses its recipient by authority;
- it leaves a lasting tie.

## 3. Being asked (the reward)

- **Who asks.** About once a year, an AI community with an open hard case writes to a posek of its own faith. Each
  candidate is weighted by:
  - Greatness band (Healthy and up only; Flourishing and Legendary count most);
  - distance;
  - same rite (preferred, not required);
  - the asker's rabbi having studied at the candidate's yeshiva (yeshiva rework slice 2);
  - an existing tie (section 5).
- **The letter.** It arrives at the leader: the case's `kehillah_din_letter_N`, with answer options built like
  `kehillah_din_sources.12`:
  - **Answer:** the reply text `kehillah_din_answer_N`.
  - **Answer from the sources:** `kehillah_din_answer_careful_N`. It is open where a rabbi of the community (the
    leader, Dayyan or Rosh Yeshiva) has mastered a work on the case's order, as `kehillah_din_source_N` already
    requires.
  - **Decline.**

  Option tooltips need new, letter-specific text. The court options' tooltips speak of the presiding judge and of the
  community's own pillars, which do not apply here.
- **Rewards for answering**, through vanilla tooltip systems and shown as sizes (memory: pillar changes as sizes):
  - Greatness: minor, or moderate for the careful answer;
  - the asking leader's opinion;
  - Learning or Talmudics experience for the rabbi who wrote it;
  - the cited work counts toward its Authoritative gate (the 2026-11-11 reviews system), once per community.
- **The expectation.** A letter left unanswered for a season lapses, with a minor Greatness penalty. A great community
  is expected to answer. This mirrors mutual aid's lapse.
- **Controversy.** A careful answer that relies on a work under dispute in the asker's rite (the machloket and cherem
  track) opens a dispute between the two communities, through the existing track.

## 4. Asking (the trade)

- **A decision** for a community whose Greatness is below Flourishing: send a hard case from its own docket to a
  posek of its faith.
- **The gain:**
  - a better ruling on that case (the reply quality, as in today's loop);
  - a minor Stability gain;
  - the answerer's opinion.
- **The cost:** the tie (section 5) runs toward the answerer, with its pull on your rite. It also carries **a gift**:
  communities sent money to the academies they asked, and the geonim lived partly on it. The gift is gold scaled by era
  (the mutual-aid era value), and goes to the answering community.
- **Why not a hook.** A hook can force a marriage, an alliance or a vote. That is far more than a question should cost,
  and it would make every answering player a creditor. The gift and the pull are the right price: real, and in
  proportion.
- **The AI** asks on the same terms, which is how the player gets asked.

## 5. The tie: "follows the rulings of"

- **What it is.** A per-community record, on the asking title: whom it follows, and how strongly. It is a small
  number that rises with each answered letter and slowly fades without them. Only the strongest tie counts.
- **What it does for the followed community:**
  - **Rite pull.** When a follower's tie is strong and its rite differs, its leader gets an event: adopt the customs of
    the followed community (`set_character_rite`, to the followed community's rite), or keep its own. This applies to
    any rite of the shared faith. The AI weighs the strength of the tie against its own Stability. A player's own
    community can be pulled the same way, but always through an event it may refuse.
  - **Synods.** A follower within range votes with the followed community at a Synod, if it attends. The Synod stays
    rite-bound, so a pulled follower may join it later.
  - **Mutual aid.** A follower answers the followed community's appeals more readily: a chance bonus beside the
    Greatness one.
  - **Students.** A follower's travelling students prefer the followed community's yeshiva, like the alumni bonus.
  - **The Sanhedrin.** The number of followers may count toward restoring it (V46). This is to be decided when that
    is revisited.
- **Display.** A breakdown line, "N communities follow our rulings". A follower sees a line naming whom it follows.

## 6. Relation to V23

V23 proposed a full responsa network with an archive of precedent. This spec is its entry point:

- the letter loop;
- recipients chosen by authority;
- the tie.

It is not a second system. V23's precedent archive stays future work.

## 9. Slice 1 as built (2026-11-22)

Daniel's further decisions: remove responsa from the Bet Din itself (done; the consult chain is gone), make asking a
**character interaction** rather than a decision, and stop it being spammed with a **cooldown**.

- **Send a She'elah** (`kehillah_send_sheelah_interaction`, religion category):
  - **Who:** a Kehillah leader, to another Kehillah leader of the same faith.
  - **The recipient must have** a rabbi in post (`kehillah_has_community_rabbi_trigger`) and Greatness Healthy or
    higher, and must hold no other letter.
  - **The cost** is the gift (`kehillah_sheelah_gift_value`: 25 × the era value), which goes to the posek's community.
  - **The cooldown** is three years (`kehillah_sheelah_cooldown`); a posek holds one letter at a time for up to 120
    days.
- **The AI** writes through a yearly pulse (`kehillah_sheelah_ai_pulse_effect`), not the interaction. An AI community
  below Flourishing has a 20% chance a year, on the same cooldown and gift. It writes to a posek in diplomatic range,
  weighted toward Flourishing (+2) and Legendary (+3) Greatness and the same rite (+1).
- **The case** is one of the 14 docket cases, at random.
- **The letter** (`kehillah_sheelah.0001`) shows `kehillah_din_letter_N`. Its options:

  | Option | When | Gives |
  |---|---|---|
  | Send the ruling | always | Greatness +10, Learning XP |
  | Answer from the sources | the leader or Dayyan covers the case's order (`kehillah_sheelah_can_cite_trigger`) | Greatness +20, more XP, minor stress |
  | Decline | always | Greatness −10 |

  Answering also earns the asker's respect, and the posek holds "Consulted My Learning" toward the asker.
- **The reply** (`kehillah_sheelah.0002`) shows `kehillah_din_answer_N`, `kehillah_din_answer_careful_N`, or a
  refusal. It gives the asker Stability +10, or +20 for the careful answer.
- **Not in this slice:**
  - the tie (slice 2);
  - rite pull (slice 3);
  - controversy (slice 4);
  - the case coming from the asker's actual docket;
  - citations counting toward a book's Authoritative gate.

## 10. Ask for Help with Your Studies; the player letter cap (2026-11-22)

Daniel: a second use for a letter, help with the work you are reading, and do not overwhelm players with letters.

- **Ask for Help with Your Studies** (`kehillah_ask_study_help_interaction`, religion category, players only):
  - **Who:** a character running the Study Torah scheme writes to a rabbi of the same faith who has mastered the work
    being read (`kehillah_study_help_knows_work_trigger`).
  - **That trigger** is generated from the library's id-to-work table into
    `kehillah_study_help_generated_triggers.txt`. Regenerate it when the catalogue's ids change; it holds 181 works on
    2026-11-22.
  - **The cost** is a small gift (10 × the era value), with a one-year cooldown; a rabbi holds one request at a time.
  - **The rabbi** (`kehillah_sheelah.0011`) explains the passage, writes out the whole argument (Learning 15 or more),
    or declines.
  - **The reply** (`kehillah_sheelah.0012`):
    - an explanation adds 60 progress (about a quarter of a phase) to the current study phase;
    - the whole argument also sets `kehillah_study_torah_mastery_bonus`, so the phase succeeds;
    - neither applies if the student has moved on to another work.
  - **The AI never uses it,** so it adds no letters for players.
- **The player letter cap:** an AI community writes to a player posek at most once a year
  (`kehillah_sheelah_player_letter_gap_days` = 365, in `kehillah_sheelah_posek_trigger`). A player's own requests are not
  capped by it.

**Live-tested 2026-11-22** (screenshots `04`-`06` in `docs/testing/screenshots/2026-11-22-sheelot/`):

- the menu names the work, and the gift and cooldown apply;
- the rabbi's letter offers the full argument at Learning 31;
- the brief reply adds scheme progress;
- `error.log` stays clean.

**Corrections:**

- Progress is counted in days of a roughly 240-day phase, so 25 was raised to 60.
- The first test reply read "moved on" because the hand-made scheme had no library, so the study tick dropped its work.
  With a library set, the reply applied normally.

## 11. One interaction (2026-11-22)

Daniel: "combine the help with studies into the responsa interaction ... I want them streamlined together." Ask for
Help with Your Studies is no longer a separate interaction.

- **Send a She'elah** (`kehillah_send_sheelah_interaction`) has two exclusive send options:
  - **A case from our court** (section 9). Shown when both are Kehillah leaders, and selected by default for them.
  - **A passage of [the work you are reading]** (section 10). Shown when the actor is studying and the recipient is a
    rabbi of the faith.
- **Requirements and costs** follow the selected option, each with its own gift and cooldown.
- **The events** stay separate (`.0001`/`.0002` for a case, `.0011`/`.0012` for a passage), since a ruling and an
  explanation read differently.
