# Bet Din citations: strength and reliability

## What is true now

- Naming a source adds a flat `kehillah_din_cite_tally` (32) to the case, whatever
  the work. The court picks among three named authorities: grounded (the highest
  standing), innovative (newer, below Esteemed) and conviction (a work whose tenets
  the judge holds).
- 2026-11-23 interim fix (commit bb987e0): the Mishnah ranks one below its standing
  when the court picks its grounded authority, and a ruling grounded on it alone
  adds 20. This becomes a special case of the system below and can then go.
- Before a case, the opening event lists the relevant works the faith knows and
  which judges have mastered each (the bench sources).

## Proposal (Daniel's design)

Every cited source has two separate values, both shown in the option's tooltip:

1. **Strength**: how much the ruling gains if the argument holds. Higher for
   **less widely mastered** works: a source few people have studied offers a
   view the parties have not already considered.
2. **Reliability**: the chance the argument holds, rolled against the citing
   judge's **Learning**. Higher for works of **higher standing**. A low-standing
   source is risky: more spread between success and failure.

So the Talmud is safe but ordinary; an obscure work can win a case outright or
undercut it.

Other parts:

- **No relevant source:** the "cite a source" option is still shown, greyed out
  (`show_as_unavailable`), so the player sees why it is missing.
- **No list in advance:** drop the bench sources list from the case's opening.
  Instead, after the ruling, show what each judge ruled and every source cited
  (the closing event or the activity log).

## Notes for building it

- **Showing the odds:** use a `random_list` with modifiers (or vanilla's duel-style
  skill check) so CK3 shows the chance itself. Do not write odds or numbers in loc
  prose.
- **"How many have mastered it":** written works already keep a counter,
  `global_var:kehillah_wk_<slot>_masters` (`kehillah_sefer_effects.txt`). Catalog
  works have none. Either add one per work, increased where a work is mastered,
  or use the inverse of standing as a cheap proxy. A real count is better: it lets
  a little-studied but respected work be both strong and reliable.
- **Failure:** decide whether a failed citation only adds nothing, or costs the
  case some of its score. A real cost is what makes this a risk, but it should be
  smaller than a success's gain at equal odds.
- **Existing effects:** the grounded, innovative and conviction effects (Stability;
  Greatness, Prestige and the unease chance; Piety and popularity) could stay as the
  flavour of each choice, with strength and reliability on top. Or they could be
  folded into the two dimensions. To decide.
- **Rising standing:** 2026-11-23 suggestion. A good ruling resting on an
  unconventional work could count double toward that work's standing, so a court
  can raise a work it keeps citing.
- **Generated code:** the per-order picks are generated by
  `scripts/kehillah_library_catalog.py`; change the generator, not its output.
