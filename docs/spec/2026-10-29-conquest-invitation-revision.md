# English conquest invitation revision — 2026-10-29

**Status:** Implemented; source review and xTiger pass against the unchanged 15-error baseline. The ruler decision, policy invitation, ruler event and scripted founding shortfall passed an [isolated live check](../testing/2026-10-29-english-conquest-invitation-live-test.md); community-side letter rendering, natural one-year timing and AI pick rate remain pending. Supersedes [V18](v18-norman-conquest-and-new-communities.md) §§1.4, 1.6 and 1.7's weighted automatic founding of four English communities. Builds on [V34](v34-outlook-teachings-and-founding.md) §7 and the [settlement law](2026-10-28-jewish-settlement-law.md).

The first transfer of England's crown out of House Godwinson still detects the conquest. It now opens a **one-time, five-year decision** for the new crown holder. William's AI weight is 500, Harald's is 100, and another claimant's is 30; a human ruler decides freely. The decision requires an independent ruler able to hold the Jewish Settlement law. It is consumed on selection.

Selection passes the existing **Encouraged** law. If the new ruler inherited Banned or Discouraged, it first sets the ordinary Allowed baseline without change effects, then passes Encouraged through the law's full `on_after_pass` path. That path issues the same 20-year open invitation used elsewhere, so settlers, exiles and Jewish adventurers use their normal invitation routes. Communities receive a special letter about England's towns and the Channel crossing, through a triggered description on the ordinary invitation event. The ruler receives a report that letters have gone out.

An eligible player adventurer receives the existing London offer promptly after the decision; its decline/death fallback remains. One year later, a hidden review counts registered communities physically based in England. If fewer than three exist, it founds at most the difference in vacant sites, in order: London, York, Lincoln, Norwich. A player newly eligible at that time may still receive the London offer if London is needed. Every founding uses the established charter and registry setup. A successor to the king receives another scheduled review if the original king dies before the year is up; the first review consumes the pending flag. This can delay the backstop after succession.

The earlier immediate-four outcome is intentionally replaced. The target of three is a floor after a year, not a cap on ordinary settlers or later growth. The direct debug path is `event kehillah_debug.130` for issuing the invitation and `event kehillah_debug.140` for an immediate simulation of the shortfall review, followed by `.131` for London state.

**Broader conquered-realm decision:** Deferred as a separate design unit. The English detection is tied to a documented 1066 succession and known founding sites. A general rule needs a reliable conquest-versus-inheritance signal, a low-control threshold, eligible sites, and safeguards against recurring AI settlement invitations; applying England's hardcoded backstop to other realms would give the wrong settlements.
