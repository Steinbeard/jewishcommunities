# Ba'alei ha-Miqra and study fulfillment — 2026-10-13

**Status: Implemented in the separate development branch; source checked, live gameplay pending.** This addendum follows [Talmud Torah](jewish-talmud-torah-tenet.md) and supersedes the idea that Karaites should progress through its rabbinic book inventory. It also records the faith-level tenet prohibitions requested by Daniel.

## Authority and study

Karaism's starting Rite exchanges vanilla Literalism for **Ba'alei ha-Miqra**. The tenet makes the vanilla Religious Scholar trait a virtue and opens a continuing, self-targeted **Examine Scripture** scheme to adult Jewish rulers, Kehillah leaders, and landless adventurers who follow a Rite with the tenet. The scheme is independent of the community's inventory of Mishnah, Talmud, midrash, and other rabbinic works. It never uses semicha or the Rabbi trait's tracks. Three successful courses give the Religious Scholar trait; later courses grant its XP. A successful course offers two choices: private reflection grants 25 Learning lifestyle XP and 8 Religious Scholar XP; sharing an interpretation grants 30 Piety, 4 Scholar XP, and +5 same-faith opinion for two years. Before acquiring the trait, both choices count toward the three-course threshold instead of attempting to add XP to an absent trait. A failed course grants 10 Learning lifestyle XP. A course takes 540 base days and uses Learning in its success chance.

The first pass models *offering* a reading to the community, with a modest opinion reward. It does not yet settle a concrete calendar or observance dispute or change a community pillar. Those should be later event cases whose outcomes can be accepted or challenged. No book list was duplicated to imply that Karaites read the rabbinic curriculum. The Karaite Jews of America's [reading room](https://www.karaites.org/reading-room.html) describes interpretation by context, analogy, and reasoning, rather than crude literalism.

## Reciprocal tenet status

On a new game's post-lobby start, `rabbinic_faith` marks Ba'alei ha-Miqra **prohibited**; `karaism` marks vanilla Religious Legal Pronouncements (displayed as **Halakha** for Judaism) prohibited. These are faith-level statuses, so they affect branched Rites of those faiths. Legalism remains shared. Talmud Torah remains available to Jewish Rites generally: studying the Torah is not itself the point of dispute. The proposed prohibition concerns rejecting binding rabbinic rulings as the source of law. Rabbanite plain-sense interpretation remains ordinary practice; [Rashi on Genesis 3:8](https://www.sefaria.org/Rashi_on_Genesis.3.8.1) explicitly seeks that reading. This limited symmetric mapping is a first pass; a future dedicated tenet for binding rabbinic authority could replace the generic Halakha prohibition.

## Spiritual Fulfillment

Vanilla 1.20.1 has a non-Christian `default_fulfillment` track, so Jewish characters can gain Spiritual Fulfillment. The mod's Learn Torah shared work effect now grants +3 (`minor_spiritual_fulfillment_value`) for a newly mastered work and +1 (`miniscule_spiritual_fulfillment_value`) for a successfully reread work. Unmastered readings grant none. These gains follow actual study and apply whether or not the reader has Talmud Torah; that tenet retains its separate Piety, Learning XP, mastery-floor, and Rabbi XP bonuses. The separate Examine Scripture scheme grants +3 on a successful course, whichever choice is taken, and none on a failed course. The Fulfillment effect is guarded to playable characters, following the vanilla 1.20.1 precedent that it errors on non-playable characters; NPC readers still receive other rewards. This is deliberately occasional rather than a monthly passive gain.

## Verification and limits

`ck3-tiger` 1.19.0 recognizes the new files but flags 1.20.1 `rite`, `set_tenet_status`, and `change_spiritual_fulfillment` syntax as unknown; all three appear in the installed vanilla 1.20.1 game files. It also flags `faith:rabbinic_faith`/`faith:karaism` because its religion model predates 1.20; installed vanilla on-actions scope `faith:rabbinic_faith` directly. The console-only probes are `kehillah_baalei_hamiqra_debug.1` (Rite and prohibition status) and `.2` (scholarship and fulfillment in a disposable save). The tenet display, scheme start and completion, three-course trait award, status widget, and fulfillment values need live verification. The faith status initializer is for fresh games; compatibility with saves begun before this tenet exists has not been established. See the [dated source-check log](../testing/2026-10-13-baalei-hamiqra-source-check.md). The shared CK3 process belonged to another session, so live boot and gameplay checks were deferred without touching it.
