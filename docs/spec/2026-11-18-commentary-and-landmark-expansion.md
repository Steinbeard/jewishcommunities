# Commentary and later landmark works

**Status: PARTLY BUILT 2026-11-18; source and xTiger checked, live verification pending.** Extends [the writing scheme specification](2026-11-17-writing-a-sefer-scheme.md). The higher mastery and direct ledger navigation sections below are proposals, not current behavior.

## Built: conviction and additional niches

Each future work whose catalog row has `argues_for` now names one `writing_thesis_tenet`. A landmark author must hold that conviction personally or belong to a rite that holds it. Other arguments in a multi-tenet work continue to affect reception but do not each block authorship. A work with no `argues_for` has no thesis-tenet gate. The project choice's landmark readiness hint uses the same full eligibility trigger as the completion roll.

Eight catalog niches join the existing 28: Ibn Ezra's Torah commentary (c. 1160), *Sefer Mitzvot Gadol* (c. 1247), *Or Zarua* (c. 1260), Ramban's Torah commentary (c. 1268), Karaite *Sefer ha-Mivhar* (1293), *Sefer ha-Chinukh* (late thirteenth century, assigned 1300), *Arba'ah Turim* (before 1340, assigned 1335), and *Maggid Mishneh* (fourteenth century, assigned 1350). The catalog now has 36 future niches after 867, 20 after 1066 and 12 after 1178. These dates are approximate gameplay circulation years. The dated fixed works may still circulate historically if no one fills their niche sooner.

## Built: commentary relationships

Catalog commentaries name their base work in their detail text. A base work's hover lists commentaries currently in circulation, so a future book does not appear before it is written or reaches its historical year. A newly written commentary increments the source's commentary count and, where the source has a standing ladder, improves its reception. The added *Maggid Mishneh* comments on the noncanonical *Mishneh Torah*, giving this rule a concrete landmark route. A canonical source has no mutable standing; its authority gives the new commentary a small reception boost. A noncanonical source at standing 6 or above gives a larger boost, while a source at standing 2 or below brings initial suspicion. These are small reception shifts, not guaranteed changes in rank.

## Proposed: deeper mastery and navigation

For a few foundational works, introduce **mastery II** after repeated reading, then **mastery III** after mastery II plus a high-standing commentary and a difficult Learning check. Keep ordinary mastery sufficient for existing decisions and let the higher levels improve study, teaching or writing aptitude. Record the commentary requirement by work, not as a generic count, so reading a gloss on another text cannot satisfy it.

The ledger should eventually offer a direct control to jump from a base work to one of its commentaries and back. The current hover list is a browseable summary, not a clickable link. Free commentary slots are also a later design step: the ordinary writing path deliberately produces an artifact only, while the current landmark catalog has fixed identities. A lasting nonhistorical commentary needs its own quality threshold, authored slot, source link, reception behavior and readable title before it can enter the ledger.
