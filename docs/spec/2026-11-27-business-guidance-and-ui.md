# Business guidance and presentation

The player should be able to answer three questions: what contributes to Affluence, which premises can be staffed now, and whether another craft would have someone to operate it.

- The Domain Affluence card and its Quarter driver tooltip include operating/built premises, unassigned eligible household count, the link from household assets to Affluence, seasonal growth and assessments, and the next useful action. Business income is not counted again as an instant Affluence reward.
- Domain has separate Households and Manage businesses controls. The latter opens the Businesses sub-tab. The Quarter utilization summary explains household assets, staffing and the Kahal House soft ceiling on hover.
- Business rows explicitly size portraits, wrap household/status text, shorten vacancy labels and move full two-year appointment terms to a tooltip. Native Appoint stays accessible so the picker can explain eligibility. Vacancies say whether an operator is currently available. Active sub-tabs remain visibly selected; the hidden sub-tab does not reserve layout space. Household cards wrap long livelihood and money lines.

## Advice and priorities

**Staff an existing business first.** A priority-170 top-bar alert requires an actually built, unoccupied native position and at least one courtier who passes native `can_be_employed_as` for that exact position. Its description identifies the first available business. Its click opens the owned Quarter through native interface effects; the utilization button selects Businesses. Domain’s Manage businesses control opens the sub-tab directly through the tested request/widget bridge. It never hires automatically. The exact native predicates also drive vacancy labels; no proxy based merely on court size, wealth, dynasty prestige or general unemployment is used.

**Consider another craft.** A lower-priority (90) planning alert requires no vacant existing business, an eligible unassigned household, peace, charter construction permission, no ongoing domicile construction, a free internal slot in an actual Craft Row tier, at least three times the base first-tier craft gold cost, and an unbuilt universal first-tier trade (dyers, goldsmiths or tanners). It opens the Quarter for Craft Row inspection. Its wording explicitly leaves the final native price and conditions to the building inspector. This is deliberately conservative advice, not a promise that any regional trade or exact quoted final cost is available. Other regional trades and building/upgrading Craft Row itself are not advertised by this first pass.

When a business is vacant but no native candidate qualifies, the player gets an explanation in the panel/Domain tooltip rather than a misleading staffing alert. One economic job per household, leading-family exclusion and existing commitment rules remain authoritative. Construction advice is suppressed until vacancies are resolved, even if the player might rationally choose a different trade instead.

Native `_important_actions.info` allows only interface effects in alert clicks. Alerts therefore use a shared `close_view` / domicile-scoped `open_view_data` helper, with an explicit player; they do not write gameplay variables. This also means an alert click opens the Quarter rather than automatically selecting a sub-tab.

The first live check exposed a Domain focus failure in the initial `OpenGameViewData` bridge. It was replaced by three stages: dismiss My Realm, use vanilla’s guarded `ToggleGameViewData` only if the Quarter is closed, then select the requested tab after the Quarter is actually open. The Households button shares this path; requests are mutually exclusive. This supersedes the older spec’s claim that a direct Open call suffices to focus an already-open Quarter.

The advice is below safety/crisis priorities. It creates no event popup, pays nothing, starts no construction and adds no new cooldown. Existing native important-action refresh/dismissal rules apply. Alert clicks recheck their predicates so stale alerts do not navigate to an irrelevant view. Domain-button request flags expire after a day and are consumed unconditionally; opening views never mutates household assets or pays income.
