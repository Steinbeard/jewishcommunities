# Kashrut

## 1. What 1.21 offers

1.21 added a faith doctrine group, `dietary_restrictions`. Its doctrines work through parameters that vanilla holding
buildings read, from their **county's** rite, to choose what food their province asks of the market:

| Parameter | Effect on food needs |
|---|---|
| `cereals_forbidden` | meat instead of cereals |
| `meat_forbidden` | no meat |
| `fish_permitted` | fish allowed |
| `diet_needs_comforts` | comforts instead of meat |
| others | similar substitutions |

The goods involved:

- **meat:** meat and fish;
- **comforts:** cheese, fruit, oil, honey and tea;
- **separately:** cattle and salt.

Every Jewish rite had **No Dietary Restrictions** ("eating other living things is not a problem").

## 2. The problem: where Jews live

Kehillot are domiciles in Christian and Muslim counties. A doctrine alone would change food needs only in Jewish-rite
counties, such as Khazaria and Beta Israel lands, or Jewish realms later (Track B). Nowhere else would a player see it.
Kashrut has to show where Jews actually live: in the **Quarter**, and in what the community trades with its town.

## 3. Representing kosher meat

Kosher meat is not something a market sells. It is made by the community:

- **Shechita:** the community's own shochet slaughters a **live beast** of a permitted kind. In 1.21's goods, that is
  **cattle**, not market meat (which, generically, includes swine).
- **Melichah:** the meat is **salted** to draw out the blood. That needs **salt**.
- **What may not be eaten is sold.** Beasts found unfit (*trefot*) went to the town's butchers, and so did the
  hindquarters in most communities, since few removed the sciatic nerve (*nikkur*). This is a well-attested source of
  both trade and friction with Christian butchers' guilds.
- **Fish with fins and scales** are permitted, so fish stays allowed.
- **Wine** for Kiddush is already one of the Quarter's needs.

## 4. Slice 1 as built (2026-11-23)

- **The doctrine:** **Kashrut** (`doctrine_diet_kashrut`, in `common/religion/doctrine_types/kehillah_kashrut_doctrine.txt`)
  replaces No Dietary Restrictions on every Jewish rite and faith:
  - Rabbinism and the three minhagim;
  - Yordei Merkavah;
  - Karaites;
  - Kabars;
  - Samaritans;
  - Beta Israel;
  - Cochin.
- **Who can pick it:** only a Jewish rite (`can_pick`, `rite_religion_tag = judaism_religion`).
- **Parameters:**
  - `meat_permitted`, `fish_permitted` and `leather_permitted`, so vanilla holdings in Jewish counties eat normally;
  - `kehillah_kashrut`, whose tooltip line tells the player what follows.
- **Character modifier:** cattle sell 2% dearer for Jewish characters. Jews were prominent cattle dealers in medieval
  and early-modern Central Europe.
- **The Quarter** (`kehillah_jewish_quarter_01`) now asks the town market for **cattle** and **salt**, beside wine,
  books and cloth. Each need met pays the host a little more artisan tax.
- **The kosher table** (`kehillah_bd_kosher_table_value`, an Affluence line), for a community whose rite keeps kashrut:
  - a market short of cattle or salt costs 15 ("lacks beasts for the shochet or salt for koshering");
  - with its own slaughterhouse in a market that supplies both, the community gains 10 ("Our butchers keep a kosher
    table").
- **The trefa trade:** the slaughterhouse (`kehillah_slaughterhouse_01/02`) produces 1 or 2 meat into the host town's
  market.

## 5. Proposals

- **Meat and milk:** the Quarter's two kitchens, as a cheese need. Kept out of slice 1 to keep the market asks small.
- **The butchers' guild:** an event chain where Christian butchers object to Jewish meat sold in the town. This is a
  natural crisis seed (the R7 track).
- **Passover:** a seasonal grain need (matzah wheat) and a shortage event. It could join the holiday content.
- **Karaite difference:** Karaites read "a kid in its mother's milk" literally. A Karaite community could skip the
  cheese ask.
