# Playing the pre-alpha and sending feedback

**Status:** pre-alpha player guide, 2026-11-16. Update with each test build; see [ROADMAP](https://github.com/Steinbeard/jewishcommunities/blob/master/ROADMAP.md) for detailed implementation and verification status.

## Before you begin

- Use **CK3 1.20.x** and **Roads to Power**. Royal Court and other DLC add some content but are optional.
- Enable only **Jewish Communities (Pre-Alpha)** in your playset for this test. If you also have a source copy, leave it disabled.
- Start a **new campaign after an update**. Save compatibility between pre-alpha builds is not guaranteed.
- The 1066 starts have had more testing than 867 or 1178. All three dates are welcome test ground.

## A useful first hour

1. Choose a 1066 Kehillah. **Worms** has a stronger starting school; [Rashi, Granada and David](three-starts.md) offer different stories.
2. Open **Domain**. Hover each pillar, inspect the Quarter and charter, then choose a leadership style if the control is available.
3. Fill a council vacancy and give a councillor a task.
4. Open the library and the Sefarim Ledger. Compare a book you hold with one you could acquire.
5. Open a Jewish map mode and inspect another community.
6. Play through a holiday, a Bet Din case or a crisis if one arises.
7. Review succession candidates. If you continue long enough, tell us whether the next leader felt like the community's choice.

## Especially useful reports

The newer leadership-style panel, Sefarim ranks and seeding, Nagid office, crisis UI and aftermath, adventurer community visits, David's two-step claim and Exilarch route, and the 867/1178 starts need more eyes. Reports of missing text, confusing requirements, off-screen controls, balance swings and late-game performance are all valuable.

When something breaks, send:

1. What happened and what you expected.
2. Your character, start and approximate in-game date.
3. A screenshot for anything visible.
4. For a crash or broken interaction, `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/logs/error.log`. **Copy it before restarting**; the game rewrites it at launch.
5. A save from before a crash or stuck state, if you have one.

You can also tell us what felt compelling or hard to understand. That helps shape the next build.
