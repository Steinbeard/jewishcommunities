Jewish Communities

Lead a Kehillah: a Jewish diaspora community within a host realm. Build its Quarter, manage its people and institutions, and continue through appointed leaders as the community changes over generations.

Version 0.1 pre-alphaCK3 1.20.xRoads to Power required

What you play

Your seat is a community title rather than a county. The host ruler controls the surrounding land; your decisions shape a Jewish Quarter, a council, a library, and relationships with other communities and the host court. The community continues when leadership changes.

The long game is organized around Prosperity (families and economy), Stability (cohesion and protection), and Greatness (learning and renown). These pillars appear in Domain and affect what the community can sustain or build.

Getting started

  1. Install the pre-alpha. Use CK3 1.20.x with Roads to Power. Enable only the shared pre-alpha copy in your launcher playset. Installation and test notes
  2. Choose a 1066 character. Rashi, Joseph ibn Naghrilla, and David ben Hezekiah each introduce a different part of the mod. Compare their first goals
  3. Open My Realm → Domain. Read the three pillar tooltips, then inspect the Quarter, council, and charter before spending resources.

Reading the Domain screen

The custom Domain view places the Quarter, host charter, and three pillar bars in one place. Hover a pillar to see its sources. Each moves through five bands, from Crisis to Legendary; stronger bands unlock higher Quarter tiers. Scholarly, Political, and Commercial leadership styles change the baseline emphasis for ten years.

About this pre-alpha

The core community, Quarter, council, and Bet Din have been played live. Newer systems have uneven live coverage; expect rough balance, missing text, and bugs. Saves may not survive updates, so start a new campaign after each build.

For a clear report, include your character, in-game date, what you clicked, what happened, and a screenshot or error log if relevant. See testing and feedback. The ROADMAP tracks build and verification details.
