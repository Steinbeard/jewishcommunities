# Bet Din live feedback from Daniel

**Status: user-reported visual findings, 2026-11-25; revised build awaits a fresh live run.** The screenshots were supplied by Daniel from his own CK3 session. Codex did not control or capture the game.

## Observed in the previous build

- The authored case option showed an empty scripted-value percentage and long, static rules prose. Native weighted outcomes subsequently displayed numeric chances in a screenshot, but also showed the engine's “Nothing happens” filler under each result.
- The citation screen showed two gray options without book names when no suitable work had been mastered.
- Personal feedback displayed a percentage and internal argument weight after the roll. The separate panel result arrived later, following other events, and mixed each judge's position with chance, weight, citation and outcome prose in one paragraph.
- The outcome tooltip listed two opinion reasons per litigant: ruling direction and reasoning quality.
- A case from the original fourteen still showed its older direct-citation button and older choice flow.

## Changes to retest

All 114 cases now use a native weighted chance preview, a concise primary-skill explanation, trait-based stress reasons, available-only citation choices, and one final ruling screen for the player's argument, the other two judges and the winning position. Outcome prose omits internal weights and percentages; the ruling's strong, fair or weak grade is explicit. Each litigant receives one combined opinion reason. The original fourteen cases keep their special resolution effects behind the shared flow.
