# Crisis response and recovery: human test steps

Use the main mod checkout and a fresh launch. Keep a save before each phase transition. The existing crisis debug events can accelerate a Granada or generic crisis; a normal game start is useful for seeing the introduction and notification order.

## Granada fresh start

1. Start the 1066 Joseph ibn Naghrilla bookmark and unpause. **Your Father's Office** should appear about seven days after the start. The poem should arrive about thirty days after the start. Check that neither scene stacks with another event, and that the crisis begins as the poem appears, before you choose an answer.
2. If **Your Father's Office** does not appear by the poem's scheduled day, the poem dispatcher should show the missing office scene first, then the poem about one day later. The office scene must appear only once. In `debug.log`, look for `kehillah_granada.0008: Your Father's Office delivered` and, only for this recovery path, `kehillah_granada.0019: restoring missing office scene`.
3. On the poem popup, open the situation before answering: Agitation should already be active. Choose an answer and confirm its progress applies once. In `debug.log`, the startup schedule and `kehillah_crisis_intro: acknowledged; crisis active` should appear. The first crisis must not emit an unset `kehillah_crisis_intro_epoch` error in `game.log`.

## Agitation

1. Open the crisis situation. Confirm the three buttons read **Call for Restraint**, **Steady the Kehillah**, and **Make Our Case**. The former Suggested Actions shortcut should not appear.
2. Open and dismiss each category without choosing. Reopen it: the third proposal should be unchanged, and no second event should stack. The first two proposals can become unavailable if their underlying action was already taken elsewhere.
3. In each category, open a proposal, check its odds, and choose **Consider another course**: the same proposals return and nothing is spent. Do this from a measure, a confirmation, Answer the Accusation and the plea for troops. Then choose one proposal in each category. Confirm it opens its own scene, spends a category exactly once, gives the displayed price/track change, and does not allow another commitment in the same category. Check that the Dayyan, Shtadlan, and Ne'eman speak for their distinct Agitation categories; Armed Host uses the Shtadlan, Rosh ha-Shmirah, and Ne'eman. With an office vacant, check the neutral fallback text. Hover each proposal for its target, cost, skill, and risk; the tooltip should replace "No immediate effects" without implying the measure has already succeeded. Compare a high-skill and a low-skill adviser, and a Brave/Craven or Greedy leader, for changed odds or stress.
4. Compare the two approaches in the second scene for each established measure: fast, watch, advocacy, town support, barricades, shelter, and silver. The certain approach should deliver the Level 1 tooltip's exact cost and result; the riskier one should show its adviser skill, success, and failure. In particular, the paid watch should cost the stated gold and start a 14-day task. A successful volunteer watch starts the same task without gold; failure gives +6 Armed Host and -5 Stability, establishes no watch, and spends the Steady category. A failed town appeal, barricade, or shelter must not be described as a successful preparation in the phase chronicle.
5. For the newer action scenes, compare **Calling on Authority**, **The Lord's Protection**, and **Before the Host's Captains** under favorable and hostile relationships while keeping the adviser's skill similar. Positive/negative ruler opinion and outlook should change the first two odds; the agitator's opinion, zeal, patience, and greed (for terms) should affect the parley. Funded options cost income-scaled minor gold and have better base odds; direct options cost no gold. Confirm the named adviser, relationship, and both possible tracks agree with the Level 1 preview. Check the newly distinct titles and scene text for aid, mediation, evidence, guards, and retreat as well.
6. Follow both a successful and failed measure. Check that the Spared/Armed bars and resulting action text agree. Try Granada and a non-Granada opening to catch inappropriate names, faith-specific clergy, or missing characters.
7. On an Agitation development (especially **A Brawl at the Well**), note both bars before the scene opens and while it is unanswered. The incident itself should add 6 Armed Host on opening, once. The option tooltips should show only their own mitigation or further escalation, using qualitative track labels rather than point counts. A successful mitigation adds its own Spared progress; neglect or failure may add more Armed Host. Compare the three brawl answers: the lord's court costs Stress and rolls Diplomacy, with a large Spared push on success or a small Armed Host push on failure; the lord's opinion and outlook should change the odds. The bet din gives 5 Stability and small Spared; compensation costs gold and gives moderate Spared. Verify a scene near 100 Armed resolves without stranded options or duplicate pressure.
8. Draw **A Hall for the Speaker**, **Carts before Dawn**, **A Shutter Broken**, **Men at the Crossing**, and **The Officer Looks Away** over separate runs. For **Carts before Dawn**, compare Martial odds with and without an organized watch, and Diplomacy odds for a Compassionate leader. Verify success/failure tooltips show the correct qualitative track and the Stability or Stress cost; the passive choice should lose Stability without granting Spared. Check the other Diplomacy rolls, the watch-dependent shop damage, and whether a ruler's prior condemnation unlocks the crossing option and prevents the indifferent-officer event. None should repeat within one crisis; if one is the last development, the phase chronicle should name it. Check a Muslim and a Christian community for inappropriate faith-specific text.
9. Over several Agitation intervals, look for **A Song in the Tavern** and **Market Whispers** as catalyst notifications without decision popups. Each should add one small Armed Host push, occur at most once per crisis, and be eligible for the transition chronicle. Confirm the normal monthly pressure notices still work.
10. Reach **Spared** with the Armed bar low, then near full. Confirm the opener recounts only real actions and that the residual wording changes. Confirm the ruler's judgment and aftermath close without entering Recovery.

## Armed Host and the attack

1. Reach Armed Host after using all three Agitation categories. Confirm **Seek Protection**, **Defend the Quarter**, and **Parley with the Host** are each available once, with their own three proposals. Confirm the old Agitation buttons are absent.
2. Inspect **Contribute X Gold (+1 Held)**. Note X and Held points; click once. Check exact gold deducted, Held +1, new X greater than or equal to the old X. Try a low treasury, the 25-point cap, and a successful bargain before subsequent purchases; previously paid points must not be refunded. Try an AI community and watch whether it spends within its means.
3. Reach **Quarter Held** with a near-full Struck bar. The toast and opener should match what actually protected people. The phase should settle and close without Recovery. If a shop or family was harmed, note whether the aftermath acknowledges it.
4. Reach **Quarter Struck** at each available threat grade (generic, Granada, crusading host; debug `event kehillah_crisis_debug.15` and `.14` set grade 1 and 4 during Armed Host). Count distinct attack scenes: target caps are 1, 2, 3, 4 for Contained through Catastrophic. Check that a font or ransom encounter does not end a higher-grade sequence early, repeated scene families, duplicate deaths, and invented book/conversion losses do not occur. Save before a forced conversion or leader death.
5. After each Quarter Struck scene choice, expect one short result toast before the next scene, which is scheduled one day later. Compare both outcomes of the neighbor, street, castle gate, and payment rolls over separate runs; the toast should describe the result that actually occurred. Check the fire, archive, shelter, escape-route, and forced-conversion choices as well. These toasts should describe immediate facts or protections, leaving final deaths, conversions, and damage to the later attack report. If a captive leader refuses, yields, or tries to escape, check that the final toast matches the outcome before succession or conversion proceeds.

## Recovery and continuity

1. After a struck outcome and ruler judgment, confirm Recovery appears with Endure and Collapse bars. Low Stability and a hostile ruler should increase Collapse pressure; a recorded refuge should improve Endure. The ruler's money request should reach the ruler as a real event and transfer only funds the ruler has. Compare an AI ruler who likes versus dislikes the community leader: opinion, outlook, compassion, justice, and greed should influence the aid choice; a player ruler should still decide directly.
2. If there were deaths, check the **Tzedakah** request. If books were lost, check the **Books** request. Confirm another existing Kehillah leader receives the corresponding event, may refuse, pays a real cost if helping, and the requester receives the matching resource/progress. The request should not repeat in that crisis. Check an AI recipient.
3. Use **Seek Relief** and **Settle the Reckoning**. Compare the communal hearing with a skilled and an unskilled Dayyan, high and low Stability, and a Compassionate/Callous leader. A successful hearing adds 16 Endure; a failed one adds 8 Collapse and loses 5 Stability. Paying income-scaled minor gold instead adds 18 Endure and 5 Stability without a roll. Confirm the tooltip, actual gold cost, and Stress agree. Confirm no automatic building reconstruction; any monetary help becomes spendable gold, and building work remains in the usual UI. A diminished community can Endure without restoring its pre-crisis pillars.
4. If prisoners are taken, choose to gather a later ransom in the attack report. During Recovery, confirm **Redeem the Captives** pays the named captor, releases only the recorded prisoners, advances Endure, and disappears. If forced converts were recorded, confirm **Speak for the Forced Converts** opens the existing petition/return chain, including an actual ruler choice where applicable.
5. Check the ruler's separate **Agitator's Fate** scene for arrest, censure, impunity, and an absent or dead agitator. If impunity persists, confirm **Demand Justice** can petition once in Recovery. Compare just and arbitrary rulers, and favorable versus hostile opinions of the community leader; justice and impunity should respond to these factors. On a hostile edict, confirm the crisis closes with a chronicle and the separate expulsion appeal/departure remains active.
6. Reach Collapse. The final event should precede the existing community dissolution flow, with no stale community registry or stranded situation. Reach Endure in a second run and confirm it remains playable and the one-year cooldown applies.
7. Kill or forcibly convert the leader with an active Agitation, Armed Host, or Recovery situation. Continue as the successor; verify phase, spent categories, paid points, loss record, and pending ruler/aid replies survive. Check that old queued events do not fire for a former holder or duplicate outcomes. Inspect the closing memory on both an original and successor leader.

## Greyed-out decision reasons

Run this in a game without `-debug_mode` as well as with it. After using Call for Restraint, hover its greyed-out Consider it button: the tooltip should read "You have not already taken this action during this crisis", with no "Has Variable" line. Repeat for a spent Armed Host action and a spent Recovery case. Then check `error.log` for new tooltip errors.

**Scope of this first pass:** the Agitation pool includes five new developments and reworked pressure and mitigation for its existing events; the old attack encounter families remain in use. A chronicle currently selects a representative recorded development and preparation from each category. The spec's remaining hostile event families, a complete chronological development ledger, every proposed political restriction and broken-promise consequence, and fully individualized memories remain iteration work. The title owns the crisis state, but event delivery, phase changes, repeated decision clicks, expulsion handoff, and leader succession require the human checks above.
