Household wealth

Families build wealth over time.

The household panel separates family assets from a character’s personal purse. Here, Light assessment is selected and the Weil household has reached 39/100 wealth. Its displayed contribution is 0.04 gold per month, collected quarterly.

About this capture

Historical UI: the four pooled assignment buttons were subsequently replaced by named business positions. The leading-family note clips in this capture; its wording was shortened afterward.

Business premises

A building needs an operating household.

The Businesses tab lists built premises and their operating households. This campaign starts at 0/4 operating businesses. The vacant dyers’ workshop displays zero dyes and zero operating income.

About this capture

Earlier revision: this capture exposed a disabled Appoint control and clipped text. Explicit native position bindings and narrower, wrapping rows were added before the final hire test.

Native appointment

Choose a Ba’al Bayit for the trade.

The corrected Appoint button opens CK3’s native picker. Rav Yehonatan is the one eligible candidate in this fresh campaign, with Average aptitude and No Cost. The household can hold one business job alongside a civic or religious office.

About this capture

After this capture, selecting and confirming Rav Yehonatan immediately changed utilization to 1/4 and showed him as Master Dyer. The tester verified that before any diagnostic refresh. The post-hire desktop capture is omitted here because it includes unrelated windows.

27 Nov 2026 · Final UI retest · 11:59

Operating workshop

The operator brings the workshop to life.

An earlier campaign shows the staffed dyers’ workshop under the Margolis household: one dye of workshop output and 0.10 gold per month of business operating income. Replace and Dismiss are native position controls.

About this capture

This is the scripted-hire campaign, not the later Rav Yehonatan campaign. Clipping in the row was subsequently addressed. Backend revocation returned workshop output and income to zero; manual dismissal and delivery into the native market remain unverified.
