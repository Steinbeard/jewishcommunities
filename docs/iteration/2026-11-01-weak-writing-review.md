# Weak writing review

## How it was found

1. A pattern scan of every player-facing loc line (library catalog, names, breakdowns and debug
   text excluded). It looked for filler, hedges, clichés, "There is" openings, told emotion,
   placeholder text, modern words and long sentences, and it scored lines for documentation
   register: *allows, unlocks, available, relevant, specific, This means*.
2. Every hit read by hand. Most hits were deliberate style and stay as they are.

## What was found

The event prose is strong: concrete, short sentences, no told emotion, no modern idiom. Most of
what the scan flagged there ("anyway", "perhaps", "rather than", "There is time…") is deliberate
style and stays.

The weak writing is in the system text, in five kinds:

- **Interface-speak in the world's voice:** "unlocks a specific source argument", "Make this
  argument available to the presiding judge", "a relevant mastered source", "Local facilities
  determine which acquisition methods are available".
- **Canned repetition:** one 46-word list of what moves a lord's outlook, copied into six
  tooltips. "The source strengthens the ruling, but the court must still decide the facts" in
  nine Bet Din cases, and "Their joint argument strengthens the ruling" in eleven.
- **Run-on requirement and effect lines:** the settlement-site and settler-destination tooltips,
  Found the Yeshiva, the Encouraged law's last bullet.
- **Vague or hedged lines:** "may be able to help… could resolve questions", "finds a
  resolution", "It establishes the frontier for future settlement", "any right it holds… is
  held on sufferance", "an important community", "a numerous community".
- **Mixed spelling:** vanilla CK3 is American (favor 457 to favour 3, defense 148 to defence 0,
  neighbor 97 to 26). The mod mixed both.

## Applied

### Outlook explanation (6 tooltips: the five outlook modifiers and the host HUD)

- Before: "A lord's outlook on the Jews moves with his faith's teaching on them, his zeal, his
  character, his learning, Jews in his service, Jews he counts as friends or rivals, his debts to
  Jewish lenders, his chaplain's sermons, crusades, and what Jewish communities have done for or
  against him."
- After: "It follows his faith's teaching and his own zeal, character and learning. Sermons,
  crusades and debts to Jewish lenders move it, and so do the Jews he knows: those in his
  service, his friends and rivals, and the communities that have helped or crossed him."
- Problem: a flat eleven-item list. The rewrite groups the man himself, what acts on him, and
  the Jews he knows.

### Bet Din: the sources and the joint arguments

- `kehillah_din_source_1..9_tt`: the shared "The source strengthens the ruling, but the court
  must still decide the facts" becomes what the source says, taken from the case's own
  responsum, e.g. "Bava Kamma puts the burden of proof on the claimant."
- `kehillah_din_source_1..14_tt`, first sentence: "Available through mastery of Bavli Nashim, a
  responsum on this case or a recorded precedent" becomes "Open to a presiding judge who has
  mastered Bavli Nashim, or to a court with a responsum or precedent on this case."
- `kehillah_din_combo_1..9, 11, 13_tt`: "Their joint argument strengthens the ruling" becomes a
  line about what the pair actually does, e.g. "A payment that can be traced is evidence; a
  rumor is not." and "Pledges set to each donor's means are pledges that will be paid."
  Combos 10, 12 and 14 already had their own lines.
- Note: the old line told the player that the combination strengthens the ruling. The new lines
  imply it but no longer say it outright. If that is too subtle, the cue belongs in a mechanical
  line generated by the tally, not in prose.
- `kehillah_din_accept`: "Make this argument available to the presiding judge." becomes "Put
  this argument before the presiding judge."
- `kehillah_din_accept_tt`: "The opinion unlocks a specific source argument at the hearing. It
  becomes a community precedent only if that argument is actually used…" becomes "The opinion
  gives the presiding judge a new argument from the sources. It becomes a precedent for the
  community only if the court uses it, and the other judges may still challenge the ruling."
- `kehillah_din_consult_tt`: "…A reply will appear in the hearing itself; you choose whether to
  cite it." becomes "Write to a rabbi who has mastered the work this case turns on. The hearing
  waits up to twelve days for his reply, and you decide whether to cite it."
- `kehillah_din_refusal_desc`: "declines to give an opinion on the facts supplied. The court
  must hear the case with the evidence and learning available here." becomes "writes back that
  he cannot rule on facts he has not heard himself. The court must decide with the evidence and
  learning it has."
- `kehillah_bet_din.0061.letter.tt`: "A named Rabbi who mastered a relevant source will receive
  the question…" becomes "The question goes to a rabbi who has mastered a source on it. The Bet
  Din waits for his answer before it judges the case."
- `kehillah_bet_din.0062.judge3_source.tt`: "The third judge cites a relevant mastered source
  during deliberation." becomes "The third judge brings a source he has mastered into the
  deliberation."
- `kehillah_dispute.0002.d.success.tt`: "…weighs the obligation to keep a promise against the
  obligation to a poor bride, and finds a resolution that leaves both families able to stand
  together at the wedding." becomes "The rabbi weighs a promise against a poor bride's need and
  finds terms both families can accept. They stand together at the wedding."

### Requirement and effect lines

- `kehillah_found_community_site_tt`: one run-on clause list becomes three groups joined by
  semicolons: "Jews may settle in its realm; a community is near, or its lord encourages Jews to
  come; and the county is settled, at peace, and has no community close by".
- `kehillah_settlers_destination_tt`: the same treatment.
- `kehillah_jewish_settlement_encouraged_effects`: one bullet that held three ideas becomes two:
  the piety and clergy cost with its modifiers, then "Cannot be enacted under the Exclusion or
  Abolition teaching".
- `kehillah_found_yeshiva_tt`: a five-clause run-on becomes three sentences, with the hall moved
  next to the founding and the Rosh Yeshiva given its own sentence.
- `kehillah_present_gift_interaction_desc`: the cost formula, "costs more for a greater lord, a
  richer lord and a richer community: less if he is in debt, more if his treasury is full, and
  more again for each gift…", becomes "The price rises with his rank and wealth, with the
  community's own wealth, and with every gift the community has sent in the last three years. A
  full treasury raises it further; debt lowers it."
- `kehillah_secure_construction_permission_tt`: "the host's permission allows the community to
  establish new Quarter institutions" becomes "For five years, the community may build new
  institutions in the Quarter."
- `kehillah_learn_torah_decision_tooltip`: "Begin an ongoing course of study… for as long as it
  continues." becomes "…for as long as you keep at it."
- `activity_kehillah_learn_torah_journey_desc`: "Visiting another community's Yeshiva will allow
  you to learn works…" becomes "Travel to another community's Yeshiva to study works your own
  Beit Midrash lacks, and to meet its scholars."
- `KEHILLAH_LIB_ACQUIRE_TT`: "Local facilities determine which acquisition methods are available.
  Only one acquisition can be underway at a time." becomes "What the community has built decides
  how each can be brought in, and only one can be sought at a time."

### Event text and notices

- `kehillah_study_torah.0004.desc`: "Someone in your household may be able to help with your
  studies. Discussing a difficult passage together could resolve questions you have struggled
  with alone." becomes "The same lines have held you for an hour, and they will not open.
  Someone in your household might see what you cannot." This matches its two siblings,
  `desc_2` and `desc_3`, which were already concrete.
- `kehillah_task_contract.0004.desc`: "…has offered a fee for goods unavailable in the local
  markets. Our merchants may be able to obtain them through contacts abroad. We must find a
  supplier and arrange delivery." becomes "…wants goods that no market here can supply, and
  offers a fee to whoever brings them. Our merchants have partners in distant ports. Someone
  must find a supplier and see the goods delivered."
- `jewish_holidays.0008.desc`: "Gifts of food and wine are being delivered for Purim… You could
  send it to a friend, offer it to a rival, or provide for someone in need." becomes "All
  morning, servants have been crossing the street with covered plates for Purim. Your
  household's basket is packed, and the messenger is waiting to hear where it goes." The old
  text was passive and restated the options.
- `kehillah_granada_believed_tt`: "His confidence in you improves, and the harm of a possible
  attack is reduced." becomes "[Emir] trusts you more, and if the quarter is attacked, it will
  suffer less."
- `kehillah_policy_lowered_msg_desc`: "keeps the charter it holds, but any right it holds beyond
  what the realm now offers is held on sufferance" becomes "keeps its charter, but any right
  beyond what the realm now offers survives only on sufferance". This removes "holds… holds…
  held".
- `kehillah_settlement_conditions.0005.single/multiple.desc`: "It establishes the frontier for
  future settlement and negotiations, while this community's particular agreement remains its
  own." becomes "It sets the bounds for new settlement and for future charters, while this
  community keeps the terms it has agreed."
- `kehillah_favored_beyond_teaching_2_modifier_desc`: "The clergy object, though the stretch is
  one their teaching or the culture can bear." becomes "The clergy object, but their teaching, or
  the culture, can bear it."

### Title and library descriptions

- `d_kehillah_auxerre_desc`: "An important Jewish community in Burgundy, whose sages exchange…"
  becomes "A Jewish community of Burgundy whose sages trade questions and teachings with Shlomo
  Yitzhaki of Troyes."
- `d_kehillah_orleans_desc`: "A numerous community and center of Jewish learning on the Loire,
  connected to…" becomes "A large community on the Loire and a center of Jewish learning, tied to
  the scholars of northern France."
- `d_kehillah_isfahan_desc`: "One of the substantial Jewish communities of medieval Iran."
  becomes "One of the largest Jewish communities of Iran."
- `kehillah_lib_detail_work_bavli_nashim`: "These late antique arguments are directly relevant to
  questions a medieval household might bring before a Bet Din." becomes "Its late antique
  arguments bear on the questions households still bring before a Bet Din." "Medieval" read as
  the narrator stepping out of the world; the panel's scholarly "late antique" register stays.

### Spelling

78 lines in 16 files now use vanilla's American spellings: color, neighbor, defense, favor,
traveler, judgment, rumor, installments and gray. Only visible text changed; nothing inside
`[...]` or `$...$`. Files under other sessions' work were skipped (see below).

## Proposed (not applied: files in active work elsewhere)

- `kehillah_crisis_risk_tt`: "This is the annualized chance of any eligible incident, not the
  chance of a massacre…" Proposed: "The chance that some incident (a threat, a restriction, an
  accusation) strikes the community within a year. It is not the chance of a massacre. …"
- `kehillah_crisis_action_watch_tt`: "…takes fourteen days, resolved on weekly updates." Drop
  "resolved on weekly updates", which is engine-speak.
- `kehillah_crisis_action_routes_tt`: "It also unlocks a prepared escape in the relevant attack
  encounter." Proposed: "When the attack comes, the families will have a way out ready."
- `kehillah_crisis_actions_hint`: "Preparations require an active warning, enough gold, freedom
  to act and no other communal preparation in progress." Proposed: "Preparations can begin once
  a warning is in force, if the community has the gold, is free to act, and is not already
  preparing something else."
- `kehillah_crisis_appeal_decision_effect_tt`: still shows "+N toward Spared" in an effect line.
  By the pillar rule (2026-11-01) this belongs in the situation window, not the decision.
- "Review it in the Situations list." (9 crisis-act lines): interface-speak repeated nine times.
  Worth one shared key, or "The Situations list keeps the account."
- `kehillah_abolish_nagid_office_effect_tt`: "Every Jewish community in the realm loses
  Stability" should give the size (pillar rule).
- `kehillah_nagid_lost_msg_d`: "you no longer qualify for it, or the realm no longer keeps it"
  is vague. Name which one happened, through two triggered descs.
- `kehillah_news_teaching_d` (applicable, but needs a scope): "Your host's church now holds a
  different view of your people" should name the new teaching. The message has the rite in scope
  but not the host, so it needs a saved host scope first.
- British spellings left in the skipped files: crisis_race (7), crisis_act (4), crisis (4),
  crisis_toasts (3), talmud_trial (9), decree (1), safety_view (1).

## Left alone, on purpose

- The scholarly voice of the library catalog and title descriptions ("late antique", "early
  medieval"). It is a reference panel and the register is consistent.
- "The presiding judge relies on Learning." and similar: a skill cue the player needs. It is not
  weak prose, though the long-term fix is a vanilla skill-check display (see the no-effects-in-loc
  rule).
- The eight shared Bet Din verdict lines ("The court gains standing from the hearing.", "Some
  objections remain, but the ruling can be carried out."). They are generic but read once per
  case. A future pass could give each case its own.
- The 649 passive constructions the scan found. Read in sample, they are almost all natural ("was
  dedicated in 1034", "is said to have been born a Jew").
