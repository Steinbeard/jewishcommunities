# Jewish Communities 0.2.0: Release Notes

**Status:** second draft 2026-11-26 for the 0.2.0 pre-alpha update to Workshop item 3841299043. Targets Crusader Kings
III **1.21 (Florin)**. Builds on [0.1.1](release-notes-0.1.1.md). Much is checked in source only, not yet in a live
game; [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) records which. Same text as the
[Steam change note](release/steam-change-note-0.2.0.bbcode.txt).

**Start a new game.** Saves from 0.1.1 may not work. Many new features have had little testing, so please report problems.

## New features

### The Jewish Quarter
- **Building slots:** choose which of the Market, Workshop, Bankers' Row and Funduq to build. Upgrading the Synagogue opens up to three slots; the Kahal House limits how many kinds you can run.
- **Annex buildings,** such as the Hospital and the Court Jew's Office.
- **New painted art** for each building level, in Western and Middle Eastern styles, with terrain backgrounds, a Western winter scene and Jerusalem skylines.
- **Quarter district:** your Quarter now appears in its host city, adds to its economy and needs goods from its market.

### Books and writing
- **Writing sparks:** mastering a book can give you an idea. Develop it, then write it.
- **Great Works:** meet their requirements to write one of 50 historical books.
- **Wear and repair:** library copies wear out. Mend them or lay them in the genizah.
- **News of new books:** readers may study them, ban them or travel to their author.
- **Missing foundational works:** a notice and a decision to acquire one.

### Law and learning
- **She'elot:** send a legal question to a respected community. Following its rulings can draw you toward its rite. Replaces the old responsa letters.
- **100 new Bet Din hearings.**
- **Judges' bench:** ordained judges sit first, and guests choose what to do at the court, such as study or seek ordination.

### Community life and money
- **Mutual aid:** ransom captives, send relief, take in refugees and appeal for help. Replaces the old loan ledger.
- **Lending:** lend to rulers in debt, demand repayment and enforce bonds.
- **Trade:** run profit routes and invest in voyages through the Funduq.
- **Court physician:** a community with a Hospital can send its physician to an ill ruler.
- **Home community:** Jewish merchants and adventurers can belong to a community and pay it a tithe.
- **Your record in office:** members judge you by how the community has changed under you.

### Faith
- **Kashrut:** Jewish rites now keep a dietary law, and the Quarter buys cattle for its meat.
- **Herald of redemption:** a Jewish path to the messianic hope.

## Changes to existing features
- **Bet Din:** pick an argument and a source, then watch each judge vote. Nearby sages are invited. Top results are harder to earn.
- **Synods:** three agenda topics (tenets, rite practice, leadership), with every delegate's vote shown.
- **Council:** new tasks, three or four for each office.
- **Leadership styles:** reward your own actions, with new bonuses.
- **Lifestyle perks:** perks meant for landed rulers now give community bonuses.
- **Yeshiva:** stands in your Quarter; former students can pay you back, and fame draws people.
- **Invitations:** the letter names the county on offer, and you answer in the letter. You may lead the settlers yourself.
- **Head of the Jews:** petition your ruler directly and see your chances.
- **Crises:** one danger rating, and an alert for each action you can take.
- **Interface:** redesigned Domain, Laws and Community Safety screens; reminders are top-bar alerts. The Prosperity pillar is now called Affluence.
- **History:** more scholars have the Rabbi trait and live in their communities.
- **Starting out:** the Kehillah start screens are the default, and communities begin without a standing army.

## Fixes
- Crisis recovery no longer loses progress from some of your efforts.
- The Samaritan High Priesthood can no longer pass to someone outside the priestly line.
- Corrected the Temple Service and Prophetic Revelation tooltips.
