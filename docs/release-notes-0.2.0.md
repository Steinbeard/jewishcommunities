# Jewish Communities 0.2.0: Release Notes

**Status:** drafted 2026-11-26 for the 0.2.0 pre-alpha update to Workshop item 3841299043. Targets Crusader Kings III
**1.21 (Florin)**. Builds on [0.1.1](release-notes-0.1.1.md) (built from `c113fa6`). Much of what follows is checked
in source and with xTiger only, **not yet in a live game**; [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) records which. The Steam
change note is [steam-change-note-0.2.0.bbcode.txt](release/steam-change-note-0.2.0.bbcode.txt).

---

## Before you play

- **Start a new game.** Saves from 0.1.1 may not load or may behave oddly.
- **Quarter buildings are still at trial prices** (about a fifth of the intended cost and a tenth of the build time),
  so that tall Quarters can be reached in testing.
- The Kehillah start screens are now the default for each start date.

## The Jewish Quarter

- Core institutions plus **chosen specialisations**: three trade sites that open as the Synagogue grows.
- New annexes for the Synagogue, Beit Midrash, Kahal House, Hekdesh (including a Hospital), Funduq, Bankers' Row,
  and Wall and Gates.
- **New painted art:** a different building for every tier in Western and Middle Eastern styles, backgrounds for
  mountain, desert, wetland, jungle and coast, a Western winter set, and a Jerusalem skyline with the Dome or the
  rebuilt Temple.
- The Quarter is now a district of its host city, enriches the town, and houses the yeshiva.
- Quarter needs: comforts and books, wine and cattle.
- **Send Our Physician** to an ill ruler, and invest in a voyage from the Funduq.

## Torah, books and writing

- **Writing sparks** replace the Write a Sefer menu. Mastering a book can spark an idea; you develop it on a writing
  screen, over one or more stages, into your own sefer.
- Fourteen more historical Great Works, with harder and more distinct paths.
- **Books wear out.** Mend them, or lay them in the genizah.
- Every new work enters the Ledger of Sefarim. Readers hear of it and may study it, ban it, or travel to its author.
- A completion report for each book you study, with revised study rewards.
- New decision and notice for **missing foundational works**.
- Forbidden books are flagged, and the tooltip explains the suspicion they bring.

## She'elot and the Bet Din

- **Send a She'elah:** ask a sage about a case or a hard passage. Communities that keep following one sage's rulings
  drift toward that sage's rite and back them at synods. This replaces the old responsa letters.
- **Over a hundred hearings** on the docket, many newly written from Mishnaic and historical sources.
- A new argument flow: choose an argument, cite a source, watch each judge weigh it, and follow the running vote.
  Sources differ in strength and reliability.
- An ordained bench (with a lay fallback), intents for the judges, and the Dayyan preparing arguments.
- Sittings now **invite** nearby learned sages of your rite instead of standing open.
- Rebalanced: a landmark docket is much harder to earn. The sitting community gains Stability; visiting judges bring
  Greatness home.

## Synods

- A shorter agenda in three parts: **belief and tenets**, **rite practice**, and **leadership and discipline**.
- Choose among the tenets your character actually knows.
- Every delegate's vote is shown, with a final roll call.

## Community, money and rulers

- **Mutual aid** replaces the loan ledger: give help, appeal for it, and remember who owes whom.
- Lending now works with the base game's loans and debtors; demand repayment and enforce bonds.
- Merchants and adventurers belong to a **home community**.
- **Invitation letters** name a real county, its promises and its distance, and are answered in the letter. A leader
  may go in person and leave the old community to an heir.
- Petition your ruler to name a **Head of the Jews**, and revise a harsh charter.
- Small trade routes for Kehillot, built up through the Funduq.
- Leadership styles now reward what the leader does, with new extras for each style.
- Kehillah leaders have no standing army.

## Council, lifestyle and crises

- **Council tasks redesigned:** three or four per seat, including pillar moments, mutual aid, the night watch,
  escorting travellers and study partnership.
- 27 lifestyle perks give Kehillah bonuses instead of promising taxes, levies and forts.
- One clear danger measure for crises, a top-bar alert for each action you can take, and adjusted recovery.

## Interface

- Redesigned **Domain**, **Laws** and **Community Safety** dashboards.
- Reminders moved to top-bar alert icons.
- Cleaner community cards and map inspector.
- The Prosperity pillar is now shown as **Affluence**, to keep it apart from county Prosperity.

## Faith, people and travel

- **Kashrut:** every Jewish rite has its own dietary law; kosher meat needs cattle, and swine are not herded in Jewish
  lands.
- The rebuilt Temple asks for its offerings, and Temple Service is learned from Seder Kodashim.
- A Jewish **Herald of redemption** can carry the messianic hope.
- The Samaritan High Priesthood passes only to priests.
- Historical Jewish scholars are rabbis and live in their communities.
- Reworked visits by wandering scholars and fellow travellers.

## Fixes

- A stray older edit had undone the Bet Din reward rework and left three rewards undefined. Restored.
- Synod headship rulings could pass without anyone being appointed.
- Writing projects could end silently.
- Large mutual aid appeals asked for impossible sums.
- Crisis recovery could drop a whole push.
- The Bet Din invitation alert has an icon.
