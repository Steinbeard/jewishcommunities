# Community crisis situations

The new non-unique `kehillah_community_crisis` situation represents one active community threat at its domicile county. The title stores its situation reference; the situation stores the title and incident epoch. The title's stage remains authoritative. Catalysts advance the visible warning, armed, judgment, and recovery phases when the existing central crisis effects change the title stage. The preacher and mob stories retain their existing clocks and outcomes.

The integration creates or repairs an instance at crisis start and on a bounded quarterly reconciliation, handles a directly armed entry, and ends the matching instance after the recovery choice, an expulsion answer, a struck-community judgment, or title dissolution. A `kehillah_crisis_situation_done` title marker prevents the quarterly pass from recreating a finished incident. Participant refresh uses the current title holder, actual sovereign, and living agitator. `kehillah_crisis_debug.5` logs the instance and scopes for a console probe. The map pin and Situations list use native presentation.
