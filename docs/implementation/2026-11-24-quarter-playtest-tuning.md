# Faster Quarter progression for player trials

Daniel asked for much lower building costs and construction times so complete art and building progressions can be tested. The shared building script values are now one fifth of the former gold, piety and prestige values (rounded to whole numbers). For example, Gates and Night Watch changes from 50 gold and 20 prestige to 10 gold and 4 prestige. The Sofer's first workshop's literal cost changes from 25 gold and 25 piety to 5 each. Every `construction_time` in the Quarter building file except the one-day neutral entrance is about one tenth of its old value: the first gate changes from 600 to 60 days, with the longest Synagogue tier from 1,095 to 110 days. The previous values remain visible in Git history. This is playtest tuning, not final economic balance; revisit it against actual community income before public release.

The treasury-full reminder threshold falls from 300 to 60 gold to match those prices. Petition for a Wall falls from 150 to 30 prestige (75 to 15 with an Archive of Privileges), so testing the licensed wall stages is also practical. The petition's host approval and cooldown remain in force; `event kehillah_quarter_debug.4` grants the licence directly for a console-only test.

The cost change does not alter eligibility. In Daniel's screenshot the community had Market, Workshop and Bankers, so it was running three specialisations against a Kahal House allowance of three. The neutral Quarter Entrance does **not** count. Gates and Night Watch would be the fourth specialisation, so it is correctly blocked. At Kahal House tier 3, Legendary Prosperity grants the fourth place; the hidden console event `event kehillah_debug.5` sets the pillars to Legendary for a test save. Wall tier 2 still needs the host's wall licence. The blocked first-gate tooltip now explains the specialisation rule directly.

The later Worms screenshot also showed the Market close to the Synagogue and a small Workshop near the right edge. Optional site 5 moves from x=49% to 54%, site 6 from x=64% to 59%, keeping both at their existing heights. The first Workshop art grows from about 255 to 310 pixels wide within its 512-pixel sprite canvas, still narrower than its later tiers, and its hit mask is regenerated.
