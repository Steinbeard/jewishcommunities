# A smaller lived-in court

Daniel's Denia screenshot showed a sharp contrast between the dense row of background houses and the largely open court around four small starter buildings. More mechanical slots would add pins and compete with the later trade sprites. This pass adds two ordinary, non-interactive residential clusters to each Western and MENA background. The first sits between the left-side civic buildings and Synagogue; the second continues the right-side house line below the entrance. Their ground points in the 1846×852 source scene are `(585,470)` and `(1490,530)`, both at 145 px height. They are background homes, not additional building sites, and therefore remain visible at every tier.

The two region-specific transparent source sheets were generated with the built-in image tool from the existing lived-in background as a style reference. The prompt requested small adjoining 11th-century homes with the same manuscript linework and subdued palette, separately cuttable on transparent ground. The builder removes stray red edge pixels, scales and places the clusters, then adds the existing props and residents. The base `*_v2.png` scenes and former `*_lived_v3.png` outputs are preserved; the new reproducible source outputs are `*_lived_v4.png`, and the game consumes rebuilt DXT1 DDS textures at their existing paths.

The [four-panel scene preview](assets/kehillah_quarter_v5_layout_preview.png) now shows a tier-1 scene with one optional trade site and a developed scene with all three. It shows that the new homes do not cover the illustrated buildings in those two configurations. They do not remove all open ground: the middle remains a small construction court for the Synagogue and later trade sites. CK3's final draw order, pin positions and regional scaling still need a fresh launch check. If the live view remains too empty, the next pass should adjust the street and courtyard composition itself rather than add more isolated residents or mechanical slots.
