# Western terrain background studies
**Status: CONCEPT ART 2026-11-25; not packed, wired or live-tested.**

At Daniel's request, built-in imagegen edited the current Western lived-in background into five terrain studies: mountains, coast, desert, wetlands and jungle. Source files are `scripts/quarter_scene_sources/kehillah_quarter_background_western_{mountains,coast,desert,wetlands,jungle}_study.png` (each 1846×852 RGB).

The shared prompt preserved the existing Western houses, central courtyard, road, entrance, people, camera and manuscript style, changing the distant landscape and marginal vegetation. Terrain-specific instructions added mountain ridges and conifers; sea and shoreline; dunes and sparse vegetation; marshes, reeds and willows; or tropical canopy. Visual inspection shows recognisable terrain and broadly preserved composition. The desert and jungle still have Western architecture, and may eventually call for matching regional backgrounds.

These are source paintings only. Image generation can subtly alter landmarks even when asked not to; the studies have not been packed to DDS, selected by terrain, combined with winter selection, or checked with the actual in-game buildings and vanilla foreground. The current preview compositor's alignment is not reliable evidence of in-game placement. Preserve the existing pins and perform the required Luna live visual check before claiming alignment.
