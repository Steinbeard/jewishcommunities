# Painted winter art trial
**Status: CONCEPT TRIAL 2026-11-25; not packed, wired or live-tested.**

Daniel asked for image-generated winter art because the scripted winter treatment turns the scene gray. Two built-in imagegen edits try the current Western lived-in background and the five-tier Western Synagogue sheet. The images preserve warm plaster, timber and colored details while adding painted snow.

- `scripts/quarter_scene_sources/kehillah_quarter_background_western_winter_painted_trial.png` is a 1846×852 RGB background study. Prompt: preserve the existing composition and all objects; add snow to roofs and ground, retaining visible roads, warm colors and manuscript watercolor style.
- `scripts/quarter_scene_sources/kehillah_quarter_tier_sheet_synagogue_western_winter_trial.png` is a 2171×724 RGBA sheet. Prompt: preserve all five tiers, their architecture and positions; add painted snow, retain warm colors and transparent surroundings.

The images are source studies, not a game-aligned preview. The model can move details despite the prompt; the building sheet has colored edge noise and its alpha and individual cells must be checked before packing. The background keeps the broad layout but its altered road and details should be checked against actual building placement. The source background was taken from the shared checkout, whose current residential placements may differ from master. No pins were changed. The game still uses the existing winter placeholder and summer buildings. Before expanding the set, establish how winter-specific building assets can be selected, pack reviewed sprites with consistent anchors, and have the required Luna tester check the actual UI. Terrain backgrounds remain future work.
