# Composed Quarter art prototype

Daniel's Western and Granada screenshots show the remaining problem with generating each building in isolation: even where palette and ink weight match, the buildings can be too similar to identify quickly and their positions do not read as parts of one street plan. This prototype tests a different production method. It extends the [fixed-layout implementation](2026-11-23-fixed-quarter-layout.md) and supersedes its assumption that separately generated civic and commercial cutouts are the preferred final art.

For each region, imagegen edited the existing empty background into **one complete scene**. The prompt required distinct forms: a broad prayer hall or dome for the Synagogue; book porch or open study court for the Beit Midrash; tall civic frontage for the Kahal House; well court for the Hekdesh; sawtooth or open-bay workshops; an arcaded moneychanger with scales; low canvas market stalls; and freestanding entrance posts. A second imagegen edit retained only those buildings on a transparent 1846×852 overlay. Compositing that overlay on the original empty background gives the [Western and MENA prototype preview](assets/kehillah_quarter_composed_layers_concept_v1.png). Both master scenes and transparent overlays are retained in `scripts/quarter_scene_sources/` with `_concept_v1` names. This is an art proof, not a game screenshot or a validated CK3 scene.

The extraction works as an art technique: the buildings are distinct in silhouette and function, and the overlay leaves the existing painted streets visible. [Level 1, 3 and 5 full-scene studies](assets/kehillah_quarter_composed_tiers_concept_v1.png) now show how the Synagogue and Yeshiva could grow in place while the other institutions keep their silhouettes. Their full masters are retained alongside the first master. Draft transparent edits of levels 3 and 5 exposed a limitation: edge extraction omitted parts of the Western Hekdesh courtyard, and the MENA Synagogue's new wings crowd its neighbors. Those draft overlays were rejected, so only the level-1 overlays are retained as usable concept sources.

The composition is not ready to wire. The Western master places the Beit Midrash left of the Synagogue and the Hekdesh at lower left; the MENA master places the study courtyard at lower left and the Hekdesh at lower center. CK3 currently shares external-slot positions across regions, so the two masters need a common site map or region-specific sprite offsets. Several prototype buildings also need scaling to fit a 512-pixel slot and its label. Each approved tier needs an individually checked cutout before packing. The empty background remains the layer shown when a building is unbuilt.

The previous individual civic/commercial sprite experiment was rejected before commit because the buildings remained too alike. This prototype does not change the playable art, building rules or slot positions. A future implementation must first establish one shared site map, then pack the cutouts, test labels and hit masks at CK3 scale, and run a Luna-only live visual check under the repository testing rule.
