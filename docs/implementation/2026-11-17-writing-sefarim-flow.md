# Writing a sefer: one work, one name

**Status: BUILT 2026-11-17; source and xTiger checked. Live UI and save check pending.** This follows the [sefer redesign](../spec/2026-11-08-sefer-system-redesign.md) and updates the shipped behavior summarized in [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md). It does not revise the underlying catalog or standing ladder.

Daniel's Rashi playtest found that the writing chain announced a random old-style book and gave an inventory artifact, while a differently named historical commentary entered the Sefarim Ledger. The completion event called both `kehillah_book_complete_effect` (which rolls a generic identity and artifact) and `kehillah_sefer_finish_written_work_effect` (which registers the named or authored ledger work). The generic identity also drove the later news and rabbi-experience path.

The writing chain now calls `kehillah_sefer_finish_composition_effect` once. It calculates the same quality tier, registers the named or authored work, and pays the established Piety, Prestige and Greatness rewards. It creates no second inventory artifact. The completion, spread, and community-news text read the actual ledger work's latest-work identity. Nearby rabbis earn experience from the chosen genre, including when the manuscript is a named work. The separate compiled-responsa path still uses its old artifact effect and is unchanged.

**Design decision:** a sefer created through this chain is represented by its ledger entry and library copy, not by a second inventory artifact. This avoids two independently named objects purporting to be the same manuscript. Its later standing, copies and scholarly reception continue to use the ledger system.

`Survey Lasting Works` is a free, read-only decision for a Rabbanite or other Jewish ruler with the Rabbi trait and a writing-capable government. It groups all ten currently implemented, unwritten named works by subject. Each title shows the requirements and whether the existing ambition trigger currently passes. The writing event still rechecks that trigger when the player chooses a named work. The guide states the rule explicitly: historical authors sometimes have shorter paths, while any qualified scholar may fulfill a work. Rashi's two commentaries, the Rif, the Ri's Tosafot and Maimonides' works have author-specific routes. The *Kuzari* has no Judah Halevi exception; any rabbi satisfying its general conditions may write it. Rashbam has no distinct named ambition in the current catalog. These are descriptions of the implemented roster, not a historical exclusivity claim.

The first draft now varies its text for a named ambition and for poetry. At final revision, the player can keep a safe draft, revise for a guaranteed quality gain and Stress, or invite public objections for a 50/50 chance of a large quality gain or a small loss. The numbers are in `kehillah_script_values.txt`.

## Addendum 2026-11-25: completion report, manuscript names, starting standing

**Status: BUILT 2026-11-25; source, unit tests and xTiger checked. Live check pending.** Daniel's playtest of an original Great Work ("Isaac's Commentary on the Talmud") found four problems:

1. **The completion event said almost nothing.** `kehillah_writing.0302` now reports what the finish did: quality and its tier, the spark it was written from, the Ledger outcome (landmark, original, not entered because the slots are full, or a manuscript only), why a Great Work did or did not become a historical landmark (below the quality gate, no open niche, or the roll missed), and the chavrusa. It shows the manuscript artifact. The finish records all of this in `var:kehillah_writing_result_*`. The old landmark-only `kehillah_writing.0301` is folded in, and its title is reused.
   - **The reward moved onto the option**, so its tooltip lists it. It used to be applied unseen in the hidden `kehillah_writing.0300`: Greatness through `kehillah_change_pillar_effect`, plus piety, prestige and, on failure, the learning XP. `kehillah_book_tier_reward_or_defer_effect` is shared by `kehillah_book_complete_effect` and `kehillah_sefer_finish_composition_effect`. The writing finish sets `kehillah_writing_defer_reward`, so the reward waits for the option. Going through the pillar effect means the leadership-style and perk boosts now apply to this gain, as they do to every other pillar gain.
   - **Landmark order:** the niche is selected first, and the landmark chance is rolled second. The odds are unchanged, but the report can tell "no niche" from "the roll missed".
2. **The manuscript had a random vanilla name** ("Fine Making Gold"). `kehillah_writing_set_landmark_artifact_identity_effect` named only historical landmarks. `scripts/kehillah_writing_artifact_loc.py` now also gives authored slots their Ledger name and a description.
3. **The news said "An Untitled Manuscript".** `kehillah_book.0006`, `.0007` and the Community News message told Ledger works from ordinary manuscripts by `kehillah_writing_landmark`, which an original work never sets. They now test `kehillah_writing_ordinary_form`, which the finish clears and sets only for a non-Ledger book. The finish also clears stale `kehillah_writing_landmark` and `kehillah_writing_original` left over from an earlier book.
4. **A new work started Celebrated.** `kehillah_sefer_place_written_work_effect` seeded standing 4/5/6 by quality, numbers written for the old scale (Obscure, Circulating, Respected). On the [one scale](../spec/2026-11-11-standing-one-scale.md), 5 Popular and 6 Celebrated are earned. Every new work now starts **Obscure**. Quality and audience set its reception inside Obscure's band (30-39: masterwork 32, famed 35, illustrious 38, minus 3 each for an unspoken vernacular or a Yerushalmi gloss; values `kehillah_sefer_new_work_reception_*`). A fine book therefore reaches Popular sooner without skipping it. A work's own floor or ceiling still binds. Existing saves keep the standing they already have.
