## Generator

Everything new is generated by `scripts/kehillah_generate_spike_communities.py`. **Edit its tables,
not the output.** It writes:
- `common/landed_titles/kehillah_spike_landed_titles.txt`
- `history/titles/kehillah_spike_titles.txt`
- `history/characters/kehillah_spike_leaders.txt` (IDs 9001200–9001239)
- `common/coat_of_arms/coat_of_arms/kehillah_spike_title_coas.txt`
- `localization/english/kehillah_spike_communities_l_english.yml`
- `common/scripted_effects/kehillah_spike_roster_effects.txt`

The last of these holds the registry and history-seed lists. They are called from the existing
lists in `kehillah_on_actions.txt` and `kehillah_community_history_effects.txt`.

## Choices made without asking

- **Founding years are conservative approximate anchors.** Salonica 50 (Acts 17); Marseille 576
  (Gregory of Tours); Djerba 500 (the community's own tradition claims the First Temple).
- **Djerba is seated in both bookmarks.** Its community survived the Almohads by tradition.
- **Denia is 1066 only**, with no loss record. Its Jews' fate under the Almohads (about 1172) is
  not documented here.
- **Mosul's Zakkai is not linked to the exilarchal House of David.** Moses el-Constantini takes
  the existing Alconstantini dynasty, an editorial link.
- **Still open:** Bari's 1178 seat (razed in 1156) and the traveller cards.
