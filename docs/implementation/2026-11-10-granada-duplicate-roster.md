# Granada's two Yosefs

The Sephardi name list and vanilla's Granada courtier use **Yosef**. Character 9000216 now uses that spelling. On a 1066 game start where he holds the Kehillah of Granada, a separate `on_game_start_after_lobby` hook silently retires vanilla character 3221, his wife `sephardi_0004`, and their son `sephardi_0005`. They remain in history but are no longer living duplicates; the mod's Yosef, wife, and son remain playable. The hook does not override vanilla's `sephardi.txt` or remove Qasmuna, whose connection to Samuel is historically uncertain. This implements the roster portion of [the 2026-11-01 court-flow proposal](../spec/2026-11-01-nagid-court-flow-and-tax-office.md#4-one-joseph-not-two); the tax office is still separate work.

Vanilla character 3221 has **no authored DNA** in the installed game's character history or `common/dna_data`. Daniel exported the generated portrait from his campaign (game ID 33321). Its 113 inheritable and appearance genes are now in `kehillah_dna_joseph_naghrilla` and the separate `bookmark_kehillah_granada_joseph` portrait. The bookmark retains its Andalusi vizier's clothing, separate portrait ID, and age for the 1066 start. The source export is `docs/data/kehillah_granada_vanilla_yosef_persistent_dna.txt`; `scripts/kehillah_apply_historical_portrait_dna.py` applies it to both records. This matches the exported appearance's genes, though age and attire affect the visible result.

The same pass incorporates Daniel's export of vanilla's dynamically generated Rashi (game ID 67827) into the mod's Rashi (9000201) and his separate Troyes bookmark portrait. It preserves the mod's full `western_beards_straight` beard and scholar's bookmark clothing rather than vanilla's light moustache and physician attire. The source export is `docs/data/kehillah_vanilla_rashi_persistent_dna.txt`. A game-start hook registers our Rashi in vanilla's `hchar_rashi` global variable so the yearly historical-character pulse does not spawn the second Shlomo in 1066–1072. It also marks vanilla's separate theology-focus historical-Rashi flag as fulfilled, preventing a later duplicate from that route. It does not remove a Rashi already spawned in an existing save.

Granada's `kehillah_safety_court_rivals` is a +25 title variable seeded for two years at start, separate from the dynamic +10 vizier-service term. Ending the crisis does not remove the two-year variable. The event hook does not alter either risk calculation.
