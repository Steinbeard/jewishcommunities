# Writing a sefer as a long project

**Status: BUILT 2026-11-17; source and xTiger checked, live UI/save check pending.** Implements [the writing scheme specification](../spec/2026-11-17-writing-a-sefer-scheme.md), superseding the survey and no-manuscript decisions in [the earlier flow build](2026-11-17-writing-sefarim-flow.md). The earlier record remains a diagnosis of the duplicate-book bug and PR history.

`Write a Sefer` opens a free discipline event. Each discipline leads to project forms with one shared scripted gate; approachable locked forms remain greyed out. Choosing a form pays Piety and starts a self-directed personal scheme the following day. At least three 320-progress phases follow: source research, an objection, then completion. A costly long revision adds a fourth phase. Phase options alter draft quality; Learning and that quality set the scheme's visible completion chance. A learned Jewish courtier can become a chavrusa, giving a chance bonus based on Learning and language. Study Torah can now ask for a chavrusa too.

Completion registers exactly one named or authored ledger ID, automatically gives its author mastery, and creates an original-manuscript artifact with that same work ID. The artifact's title dispatch reads the work ID, and the authored-slot recycler protects a slot while its manuscript survives. A failed attempt yields Learning experience but registers no work. The completion event distinguishes failure, ordinary work and landmark work. The landmark artifact uses CK3's top `illustrious` rarity plus a distinct manuscript modifier; the ledger's rite-specific reception remains separate.

All ten preexisting named ambitions are reachable only as exceptional outcomes of the appropriate form. The Rashi Torah niche additionally needs six mastered Parshanut works, two Tanakh rounds and 65 Parshanut Rabbi XP; the Rashi Bavli niche needs four mastered Bavli orders. Historical identity affects some old ambition routes but never reserves the slot. The two Rashi display titles use the actual writer after a newly written niche is filled and the historical name for a seeded work. A filled niche leaves the generic form available. Yerushalmi commentaries can target an order the author actually mastered; their authored work begins with lower standing, while subsequent citations can improve the source work's standing.
