[h1]Jewish Communities: Pre-Alpha[/h1]

[b]Private test build. Expect bugs. Saves may not carry over between updates.[/b]

Lead a [b]Kehillah[/b], a self-governing Jewish community inside a Christian or Muslim realm. You hold no land: your seat is the Jewish Quarter of your host's city. Build the Quarter, appoint a council and a rabbinic court, gather and study books, train and ordain rabbis, and keep the community alive through its host's favour, through crises and through changes of leadership.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crusader Kings III 1.21.x (Florin)
[*]No DLC required. Roads to Power is no longer needed: since 1.21 the landless adventurer is in the base game.
[*]Play without other mods for now
[/list]

[h2]Start here[/h2]
Pick a Kehillah start screen: [b]867 (Age of the Geonim)[/b], [b]1066 (Age of the Rishonim)[/b] or [b]1178 (The Paths of Torah)[/b]. For a first game, play [b]Isaac ha-Levi, Rav of Worms, 1066[/b].

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A landless Jewish government.[/b] Leaders are appointed, not born; when yours dies, continue as the community's choice.
[*][b]Prosperity, Stability and Greatness:[/b] three pillars, from Crisis to Legendary.
[*][b]The Jewish Quarter:[/b] synagogue, beit midrash and yeshiva, countinghouse, mikvah, hekdesh and more: core institutions plus the specialisations you choose, each with painted art that grows.
[*][b]A council and court:[/b] Shtadlan, Gabbai, Rosh ha-Shmirah, Ne'eman and Dayyan, plus householder families.
[*][b]Rabbis, books and yeshivot:[/b] about 170 historical works, a Sefarim Ledger per rite, ordination, writing your own sefer from sparks of study.
[*][b]The Bet Din:[/b] judge cases, send and answer she'elot, hold synods.
[*][b]Your host:[/b] charters, settlement policy, invitations, expulsion, loans, mutual aid and contracts.
[*][b]Crises as a race:[/b] when danger comes, every point for and against the community is visible, and your actions decide whether the quarter is spared, held or struck.
[*][b]Rites and tenets,[/b] Karaite communities, holidays, Jewish map modes, and lineages from Kohen to the House of David.
[/list]
