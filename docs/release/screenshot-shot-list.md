# Pre-alpha screenshot shot list

**Status:** 2026-11-16. The 12 screenshots in `docs/images/` are from **2026-10-18**. They show
the Worms soak game from before the crisis race, the library redesign, the Jewish map modes,
leadership styles and the 867/1178 rosters. Still usable: the Quarter map, the council,
decisions, holiday and accusation events, the Bet Din and succession. **Re-shoot:**
`library.jpg` and `realm-pillars.jpg`, because the library and Domain screens have changed
since.

Capture at native resolution and save as JPG (quality about 85) into `docs/images/`.
**16:9 for the Steam gallery.** Steam shows the first image as the main preview.

## Tips

- Hide the tooltips you don't want: hover off before pressing the screenshot key.
- Pick moments with real names and numbers on screen, not empty states.
- After capturing, replace the `realm-pillars.jpg` and `library.jpg` references in README.md, and
  regenerate `docs/release/thumbnail.png` from the best new image if it's stronger than the
  Troyes Quarter.
