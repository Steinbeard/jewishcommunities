[h1]0.2.0 pre-alpha (CK3 1.21)[/h1]
[b]Start a new game. Saves from 0.1.1 may not work. Many new features have had little testing, so please report problems.[/b]

[h2]New features[/h2]

[h3]The Jewish Quarter[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Building slots:[/b] choose which of the Market, Workshop, Bankers' Row and Funduq to build. Upgrading the Synagogue opens up to three slots; the Kahal House limits how many kinds you can run.
[*][b]Annex buildings,[/b] such as the Hospital and the Court Jew's Office.
[*][b]New painted art[/b] for each building level, in Western and Middle Eastern styles, with terrain backgrounds, a Western winter scene and Jerusalem skylines.
[*][b]Quarter district:[/b] your Quarter now appears in its host city, adds to its economy and needs goods from its market.
[/list]

[h3]Books and writing[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Writing sparks:[/b] mastering a book can give you an idea. Develop it, then write it.
[*][b]Great Works:[/b] meet their requirements to write one of 50 historical books.
[*][b]Wear and repair:[/b] library copies wear out. Mend them or lay them in the genizah.
[*][b]News of new books:[/b] readers may study them, ban them or travel to their author.
[*][b]Missing foundational works:[/b] a notice and a decision to acquire one.
[/list]

[h3]Law and learning[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]She'elot:[/b] send a legal question to a respected community. Following its rulings can draw you toward its rite. Replaces the old responsa letters.
[*][b]100 new Bet Din hearings.[/b]
[*][b]Judges' bench:[/b] ordained judges sit first, and guests choose what to do at the court, such as study or seek ordination.
[/list]

[h3]Community life and money[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Mutual aid:[/b] ransom captives, send relief, take in refugees and appeal for help. Replaces the old loan ledger.
[*][b]Lending:[/b] lend to rulers in debt, demand repayment and enforce bonds.
[*][b]Trade:[/b] run profit routes and invest in voyages through the Funduq.
[*][b]Court physician:[/b] a community with a Hospital can send its physician to an ill ruler.
[*][b]Home community:[/b] Jewish merchants and adventurers can belong to a community and pay it a tithe.
[*][b]Your record in office:[/b] members judge you by how the community has changed under you.
[/list]

[h3]Faith[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Kashrut:[/b] Jewish rites now keep a dietary law, and the Quarter buys cattle for its meat.
[*][b]Herald of redemption:[/b] a Jewish path to the messianic hope.
[/list]

[h2]Changes to existing features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Bet Din:[/b] pick an argument and a source, then watch each judge vote. Nearby sages are invited. Top results are harder to earn.
[*][b]Synods:[/b] three agenda topics (tenets, rite practice, leadership), with every delegate's vote shown.
[*][b]Council:[/b] new tasks, three or four for each office.
[*][b]Leadership styles:[/b] reward your own actions, with new bonuses.
[*][b]Lifestyle perks:[/b] perks meant for landed rulers now give community bonuses.
[*][b]Yeshiva:[/b] stands in your Quarter; former students can pay you back, and fame draws people.
[*][b]Invitations:[/b] the letter names the county on offer, and you answer in the letter. You may lead the settlers yourself.
[*][b]Head of the Jews:[/b] petition your ruler directly and see your chances.
[*][b]Crises:[/b] one danger rating, and an alert for each action you can take.
[*][b]Interface:[/b] redesigned Domain, Laws and Community Safety screens; reminders are top-bar alerts. The Prosperity pillar is now called Affluence.
[*][b]History:[/b] more scholars have the Rabbi trait and live in their communities.
[*][b]Starting out:[/b] the Kehillah start screens are the default, and communities begin without a standing army.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crisis recovery no longer loses progress from some of your efforts.
[*]The Samaritan High Priesthood can no longer pass to someone outside the priestly line.
[*]Corrected the Temple Service and Prophetic Revelation tooltips.
[/list]
