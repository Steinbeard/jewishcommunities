[h2]0.2.0 pre-alpha (CK3 1.21)[/h2]
[b]Start a new game: 0.1.1 saves may not load properly. Many new features have had little or no in-game testing, so please report what breaks.[/b]

[b]The Jewish Quarter[/b]
[list]
[*]Core institutions plus chosen specialisations, with new annexes including a Hospital and Wall and Gates
[*]New painted buildings for every tier, terrain, coast and winter backgrounds, and a Jerusalem skyline
[*]The Quarter is now a city district that enriches the town and houses the yeshiva
[*]Send Our Physician to a sick ruler; invest in voyages from the Funduq
[*]Buildings are still at cheap, fast trial prices
[/list]
[b]Books and writing[/b]
[list]
[*]Writing sparks: studying books gives you ideas to develop into your own sefer
[*]14 more historical Great Works
[*]Books wear out: mend them or lay them in the genizah
[*]New works enter the Ledger; readers may study them, ban them or travel to the author
[*]Completion reports, revised study rewards, notices for missing foundational works
[/list]
[b]She'elot, Bet Din and synods[/b]
[list]
[*]Send a She'elah to a sage; communities that follow one drift toward that rite (replaces responsa letters)
[*]Over 100 hearings, with arguments, citations, judges' votes and an ordained bench
[*]Sittings invite nearby sages; landmark dockets are much harder to earn
[*]A shorter synod agenda (tenets, practice, leadership) with every delegate's vote shown
[/list]
[b]Community and rulers[/b]
[list]
[*]Mutual aid replaces the loan ledger; lending uses the base game's loans
[*]Merchants and adventurers have a home community
[*]Invitation letters name a real county; leaders can go in person
[*]Petition for a Head of the Jews; revise a harsh charter
[*]Trade routes through the Funduq; reworked leadership styles; no standing armies
[/list]
[b]Council, perks, crises and interface[/b]
[list]
[*]Redesigned council tasks, 3-4 per seat
[*]27 lifestyle perks now give Kehillah bonuses
[*]One danger measure for crises, with an alert for each action available
[*]New Domain, Laws and Community Safety dashboards; reminders as top-bar alerts
[*]The Prosperity pillar is now called Affluence
[/list]
[b]Faith and people[/b]
[list]
[*]Kashrut for every Jewish rite
[*]The rebuilt Temple asks for offerings; Temple Service is learned from Seder Kodashim
[*]A Jewish Herald of redemption; Samaritan High Priests must be priests
[*]Historical scholars are rabbis and live in their communities
[/list]
[b]Fixes:[/b] Bet Din rewards, synod headship appointments, writing projects ending silently, oversized aid appeals, crisis recovery.
