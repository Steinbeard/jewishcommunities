# Demo plan and readiness check

## Verdict

**Ready to share a guided demo of the core loop. Not ready to hand someone the game and let them play unguided.**

- **What a guided demo can honestly show:** the Kehillah government, the three pillars, the Jewish Quarter, the library and yeshiva, a Bet Din sitting you host yourself, the host charter, a succession, and a couple of authored crisis scenes. All of these were played through in the two 20-year soaks (2026-10-15 and 2026-10-16) with no crash.
- **What an unguided player would hit:** long quiet stretches (about 3 mod events a year for Worms), a gold economy with nothing to buy, pillars that never dip, no AI community ever hosting a Bet Din, and a handful of visible label and art defects.
- **Do not demo yet:** the Sanhedrin / Gaon chain (V46-V47). It was built and first passed live *today*, and its own "still to check" list is open.

## Do the systems work together?

**Short answer: each system works; the seams are mostly untested, and one big seam is known broken.**

What the evidence supports:

| Seam | Evidence | Read |
|---|---|---|

| Succession <-> charter <-> player continuity | Three clean successions across the two soaks; charter renewed 3 days after (10-16); "Continue as" works | Works. Caveat: succession wiped gold 504 to 27, prestige 2193 to 468, piety 4627 to 148 (bug 12) |
| Yeshiva <-> semicha <-> Dayyan <-> Bet Din | Graduations 7+, ordination, a full player-hosted sitting with four cases | Works |

| Host charter <-> settlement policy <-> expulsion | Live-tested 09-23/09-24, 10-12 | Works (not re-seen in the Worms soaks) |
| Pressures / accusation / disputation <-> host outlook | One accusation and one disputation in 20 years; the pogrom/mob chain never fired for Worms | **Thin.** Soaked separately (pogroms fell 37 to 9 in 30 years), but not in a full playthrough with the rest running |
| **AI communities <-> Bet Din (and any activity)** | 0 AI sittings in 20 years; every mod-side gate passes; the engine drops the plan silently, and the same happens for vanilla landless governments in a controlled test (10-18) | **Known broken, root cause unfound.** The world outside the player's own community is quiet |
| Economy <-> everything | Income +3.0/month all game; nothing worth saving for (balance section, 10-16) | Weak |

**The biggest gap is not a bug, it is that nobody has played the current build end to end.** The last 20-year soaks ran on `ab31f90` and `3270c60` (10-15, 10-16). Since then V45, V46, V47, the lineage traits and the AI-goals policy change (`use_goals`, `use_legends` and `perform_religious_reformation` set to yes, implementation doc section 19) landed. Section 19 enabled AI reformation for community leaders and no log tests it. A fresh soak is the single best readiness test.

## Fix before showing (small, high visibility)

Taken from the 10-16 report's open list. In rough order of how much they embarrass a demo:

1. **Succession wipes resources** (bug 12). Visible in any demo that includes a death. Decide: intended reset, or fix.
2. **"Tributary Invalidated" toast on succession.** Same moment, reads as an error.
3. **Label and level mismatches** (bug 18): "Yeshiva (Level 3)" offering "Upgrade to Level 3"; the truncated "Great House of the Commu..."; level not updating during an upgrade. These are on the Quarter screen you will be showing.
4. **Missing court icons** (bug 16, fixed 10-16, never seen in game). Confirm they render; if the magenta checkerboard is still there, skip the Court panel.
5. **Host outlook sign** (bug 15): the friendliest band shows -17 on the Stability tooltip. Either explain it or fix it before anyone hovers there.
6. **Vanilla text leaking in** (bug 19): the "Convene the Bet Din" decision shows East Asian magistrate art, and the planner uses vanilla magistrate text. Show the Bet Din from the sitting, not from the decision card.
7. **Bet Din ruling closing boilerplate** (still unfixed): fine for one case, repetitive over two.

Not worth fixing for a demo, but worth knowing: polygamy event re-fires every 2 Jan from 1082 (vanilla), holiday dates are not calendar-true (bug 14), the Worms bookmark shows a grey map for a few seconds (bug 20).

## The demo (about 20 minutes)

**Setup.** Fresh boot, 1066 Worms bookmark, Isaac ben Eliezer ha-Levi. Pre-flight: `ck3-tiger` clean on touched files, `error.log` baseline taken. Have a save at game start and one a few days after the Rhineland synod (15 Oct 1066) so a stall costs nothing. Expect the known multi-minute boot stall; boot before the audience arrives. Use a window at least 2064x1150; two buttons (Send Out Settlers, Host a Sitting) lacked a visible confirm at that size (bug 17).

**Run in order.** Each beat names the screen, what to say and the risk.

1. **What a Kehillah is** (2 min). Government panel and the three pillars. Hover Prosperity / Stability / Greatness; the tooltip breakdowns are the strongest UI in the mod. Show the band effects. *Risk: the -17 host-outlook line (item 5).*
2. **The Jewish Quarter** (3 min). Map view of the Quarter: Synagogue, Yeshiva, Countinghouse, Hekdesh. Start an upgrade and show the construction art. Mention the nine building lines and band gates. *Risk: the label mismatches (item 3).* Do not wait for the upgrade to finish (1.5-2 years).
3. **Library and yeshiva** (3 min). Open the Library, books tab and yeshiva tab, corpus view. Start Learn Torah on a held work. Show the yeshiva graduation / semicha event if one is queued. *Risk: ghost text on the Yeshiva tab, and a leaking Countinghouse tooltip on hover (bug 18). Click, don't hover.*
4. **Convene a Bet Din** (5 min). Plan the Sitting from the Quarter, host it, and play one case through, ideally The Schoolroom Next Door or The Butcher's Knife (both rated strong). Point out that judges with the right texts argue better. Show the ruling matches the option chosen (fixed 10-16, not replayed). *Risk: the shared closing boilerplate; stop after one case.*
5. **The host and the crisis** (4 min). Show the charter and settlement policy. Then trigger the Accusation scene rather than wait for it (rated strong): fire the accusation event from the console (`event <id>`; I did not confirm its ID, and `events/kehillah_debug_events.txt` has pillar-band setters but no accusation entry I could find), or load a save placed just before it. Show the "After the Accusation" variants differ by the route you took. *Risk: random waits. Do not rely on one firing during the demo.*
6. **Succession** (3 min). Kill the leader from the console (the exact command is in the succession runbook, `docs/testing/succession-console-runbook.md`; not re-checked here). Show the death screen (communal wording), "Continue as", and the Charter Renewed event. Be ready to explain the resource reset (item 1).
7. **Optional, only if asked:** the 1066 synod (fires 15 Oct; renews Gershom's ordinances and founds Minhag Ashkenaz). This one passed live on 10-19 and is a good conversation piece for the faith side.

**Leave out:** the Sanhedrin and Gaon decisions, the Petition for Land, the Popular Crusade and mob chain (a long wait for a natural trigger), AI community behaviour, the Court panel unless item 4 is confirmed, and anything touching gold balance. Rashi and Yosef (Rouen) starts exist but have had no soak at all; Rashi's save was used as a test host and killed on 10-19, so do not promise either.

- [ ] `ck3-tiger` run on the current head. The last full-checkout count was 0 fatal, about 987 errors, about 140 warnings, mostly the known 1.19-linter / 1.20-game `rite` mismatch; confirm no new errors on touched files.

- [ ] A save placed immediately before each crisis beat (Accusation, death).

## Longer-term, before wider sharing

These are the real blockers to "let people play it", and they are design work, not polish. They are already on the roadmap; this list is only the order they matter in for an outside audience.

1. **Find out why no AI community hosts anything.** Without it the world around the player is static. The 10-16 and 10-18 probes narrowed it to a landless-government scheduling distinction; the fallback is a mod-side yearly pulse that resolves an off-screen AI sitting. That needs a decision from Daniel.
2. **Give gold a sink and let the pillars dip.** The soaks showed Prosperity Healthy, Stability Flourishing and Greatness Legendary from about 1074 onwards. The pillar system is the pitch, and it needs to be able to hurt.
3. **Raise the event rate for the player.** About one holiday event every two years against a designed ~60% a year (V44), and 4 incidents in 20 years.
4. **Re-soak after each of the above**, on a different start (Rashi at Troyes) as well as Worms.
