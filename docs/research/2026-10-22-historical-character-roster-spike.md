# Historical character roster spike — 22 October 2026

**Status: research and tracker only; no character, title, dynasty, or startup scripts changed.** This records Daniel's 2026-10-22 request to make the 1066 world more historically populated and fun to play. It extends the existing [V43 Granada family](../spec/v43-after-the-violence-and-granada.md) and [V48 world roster](../spec/v48-world-kehillot-founded-legacy-and-pillar-ui.md); it does not supersede their mechanics. The editable person-by-person queue is [historical-character-tracker.csv](../data/historical-character-tracker.csv).

## How to maintain the tracker

Give every person a stable `HC` key. Keep one row per person, including already-built characters and speculative ideas, so an implementation pass can update `status` and `history_id` rather than quietly dropping a lead. `existing` means present in the checked `origin/master` baseline of 2026-10-22, `planned` means a concrete addition (including a plausible invention), and `research` means an unresolved identity or placement. Each row records the play hook, sources, and exactly where game content exceeds the evidence. **The uncertainty labels and accuracy caveats are development notes, not game text.** Every implemented person gets an ordinary personal name, coherent family links, and an immersive place in the world. Historical titles belong to people known to have held them; invented relatives can have plausible court roles. When a PR adds someone, update that row in the same PR and cite the implementation path in `gameplay_option` or an added column. The CSV is UTF-8 and all columns are text, including IDs, to preserve dates and leading digits.

This tracker covers the 1066 Granada / Córdoba / Lucena / Maghreb and Ashkenaz families discussed in this session, plus the proposed 867 Bodo. It is not a claim to catalog every Jewish historical figure in the mod. The older Babylonian leadership dataset remains in [its own staged source map](../spec/historical-jewish-leadership-roster.md). The as-yet-unmerged V50 bookmark work is not counted as present in this branch's `origin/master` baseline.

## Sousse: story location within the Mahdia community

A [Geniza letter from Labrat of Mahdia](https://geniza.princeton.edu/en/documents/846/) places Nissim Gaon and other Kairouan refugees in **Sūsa (Sousse)** around 1057 or 1061; Nissim died in 1062. Sousse supplies a grounded refuge and scholarly memory for the region. Labrat, independently attested as Mahdia's chief judge, keeps its 1066 seat. Daniel chose one combined Mahdia community rather than a separate Sousse title on 2026-10-23.

The installed CK3 1.20 map has `b_susa` **inside `c_mahdiya`** (`game/common/landed_titles/00_landed_titles.txt`). Mahdia's Kehillah already uses `c_mahdiya` as its capital/host. Keep a single title. Record Nissim's earlier Sousse refuge and put his fictional brother Yehuda, nephew Yaakov, and young grand-nephew Nissim in Labrat's Mahdia household. The nephew's wife Miriam is a fictional daughter of Hananel and granddaughter of Chushiel, connecting two documented Kairouan scholarly lines through a plausible but unattested marriage. Their kinship and names are documented as inventions in the CSV; the game should present them as ordinary characters, without qualification labels.

## Families and the impossible-age bug

The repository already has Shmuel HaNagid, Nissim, Joseph's unnamed wife (assigned the fictional name Hannah), and Azariah. The next Granada pass should add the Ibn Ezra brothers around their father Jacob, plus Joseph's 1065 client Isaac Albalia. Approximate 1060 is a defensible *game birth* for six-year-old Moshe, but [Cambridge dates him around 1055](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/literature-of-alandalus/moses-ibn-ezra/371DB5AA0479B270F235FD717B2A74F6), while the older [Jewish Encyclopedia gives about 1070](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/7989). Keep that uncertainty in the character file.

Rashi's father Yitzhak and his maternal uncle Simeon the Elder are attested. His mother's personal name is not secure, so use **Sarah bat Yitzhak** for her. Use **Rivka** for his wife, a name found in later accounts, and give her a 1065 death in character history. This makes the [documented 1065 return from Worms to Troyes](https://schumstaedte.de/en/shum/traditions-and-tales/) the opening to a young widower's household. Yocheved and Miriam are well-supported daughters; [Rachel is less certain](https://schechter.edu/did-rashi-dictate-responsa-to-his-daughter/). For gameplay, start all three as his and Rivka's children: Yocheved born 1058, Miriam 1060, Rachel 1063 (about eight, six, and three in September 1066). These names, dates, Rivka's death, and Rashi's widowhood are recorded with their uncertainty in the CSV, never as disclaimers in the character UI. Do not assign the daughters' later marriages at game start.

The **jewel legend** says Yitzhak refused to let a precious gem be used for Christian religious ornament, cast it into the sea, and then heard a heavenly promise of a learned son. Some retellings make the gem a pearl and give Rashi's mother an active role; another story says a wall in Worms sheltered her while pregnant. These are later traditions about Rashi's birth, not contemporary evidence. They can inspire a remembered family anecdote or event, without treating the sea voyage, miracle, or a specific physical jewel as established history. See the [Encyclopaedia Judaica account](https://jewishvirtuallibrary.org/jsource/judaica/ejud_0002_0017_0_16452.html) and [Maurice Liber's retellings](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/3165/pg3165-images.html).

The user-observed children older than their parents have a specific source in [`kehillah_seed_community_effect`](../../common/scripted_effects/kehillah_scripted_effects.txt): when a leader lacks an **adult** child, it creates one aged **26–38** with that leader as father. A [2026-09-27 live log](../testing/2026-09-27-heartbeat8-live-test-log.md) explicitly records such a child for 26-year-old Rashi. Adding three *young* historical daughters will not suppress this branch, because its condition is `any_child = { is_adult = yes }`. The follow-up must repair the succession-candidate floor and check every start leader; simply seeding Rashi's daughters would leave the age bug. This touches succession and needs the [implementation document's crash history](../implementation/v1-kehillah-implementation.md) and a delegated live playtest.

## Names, arms, mottos, and offices

`dynn_BenDavid` is currently localized **“of the House of David”**; change it to **“Ben David”** in the dynasty/localization unit. The checked `origin/master` has custom arms for HaLevi (menorah), Yitzhaki (vine), and Naghrilla (pomegranate), but **none yet for Ben David**. A lion of Judah design is a fitting proposed mod emblem, not a documented family coat of arms. [Genesis 49:10](https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.49.10) supplies the proposed motto, “The scepter shall not depart from Judah.” First check CK3's installed dynasty/house motto format and whether that motto is exposed for this mod's dynasty; if not, use it in localization or character flavor rather than claiming a mechanical house motto. Review historically named families for coherent arms, but do not imply that modern heraldic designs are documented medieval family symbols. Any new icon is mod art.

For office history, distinguish community government titles from real rabbinic positions. Nissim was a Kairouan academy head and a refugee at Sousse; Labrat was Mahdia's judge; Shmuel was Granada's vizier and Nagid. A history entry or nickname may convey those facts without falsely assigning a currently held Kehillah title. The [Jewish Encyclopedia](https://jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/7992-ibn-ghayyat-isaac-ben-judah) says Isaac ibn Ghiyyat was elected to head Lucena after Joseph's death in late 1066, whereas the mod starts him there in September. Keep that deliberate compression visible when adding his son Judah.

## Suggested build order after this research unit

1. Repair the generated-adult-child rule and add Rashi's parents, deceased wife Rivka, and three young daughters. Verify start ages and succession in game. Keep the invented names and widowhood caveats in development notes, not in character text.
2. Populate Granada's Ibn Ezra and Albalia households, then Lucena's Ibn Ghiyyat son and Córdoba's earlier academy genealogy. Link only sourced kinship; invented marriages or offices are allowed when labelled.
3. Add Jacob ben Nissim and the Kairouan scholarly lineage, Labrat's father, and the named Nissim/Chushiel descendants at Labrat's Mahdia court. Represent Sousse as their nearby refuge within the combined community.
4. Give the Ben David dynasty its short name and a sourced motto if CK3 supports the format. Extend arms and mottos family by family with explicit art/design notes.
5. Put Bodo–Eleazar in an 867 historical or playable context, not 1066. Last attestation is 847, so 867 survival is a conscious extension. The named “four captives” number only three in the underlying tradition; the fourth stays unidentified, and the capture narrative is disputed.

This order is a proposed roster workstream alongside ROADMAP's existing current-phase work, not a declaration that V50's pending live verification has passed.
