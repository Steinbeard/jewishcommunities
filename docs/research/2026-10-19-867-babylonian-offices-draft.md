# Draft: the Babylonian offices in 867 (for an 867 start, V47 phase C)

**Status:** research draft for Daniel's review, 2026-10-19. Nothing is built from this. Every name
needs checking against a scholarly list, ideally the dated tables built on Sherira Gaon's
*Iggeret*, before it enters character history. Gaps are marked; none are filled by guesswork.

## Gaon of Sura in 867

- **Amram ben Sheshna (Amram Gaon)**, compiler of *Seder Rav Amram*, the first complete
  prayer book. Died 875 at Sura, per
  [Wikipedia: Amram ben Sheshna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amram_ben_Sheshna).
- Common modern lists put his tenure at about 857-875. The start year is **not** confirmed by
  the sources found.
- Fits V47: in 867 he is Sura's Gaon, the senior academy's head, and Rabbinism's head of rite.

## Gaon of Pumbedita in 867

- **Paltoi ben Abaye:** gaon 841/842 to 857/858, per
  [JE: Paltoi b. Abayi](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/11879) and
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paltoi_ben_Abaye). Before 867.
- **Menahem ben Joseph ben Ḥiyya:** gaon 858-860, per
  [JE: Menahem b. Joseph b. Ḥiyya](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/10629). His rival
  was **Mar Mattithiah b. Rabbi**, "his superior in Talmudic lore".
- **Mattithiah b. Rabbi:** the likely gaon in 867, succeeding after Menahem died. **No dates
  were found.** Common lists give about 860-869. Unconfirmed.
- Note: in 867 Pumbedita is still at its own site. It moved to Baghdad about 890 (per this
  mod's own V29 notes).

## Exilarch in 867

- **Unconfirmed.**
  - **Judah ben David** is given as exilarch about 840-857
    ([Wikipedia: Mar Ukba ben Judah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar_Ukba_ben_Judah)).
  - **Zakkai ben Ahunai** died in 890 as exilarch; his nephew Mar Ukba succeeded him.
  - The holder in the 860s was not found.
- The 9th-century *Seder Olam Zuta* is the primary source for the line
  ([Iranica: Exilarch](https://iranicaonline.org/articles/exilarch)).
- Candidates to check: Zakkai ben Ahunai's start year, and any exilarch between 857 and
  Zakkai.
